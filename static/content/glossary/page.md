<div class="text-2xl text-success-0 mt-3">Common RP and community terminology used across Shadows of Vardoran.</div>

> [!WARNING] Committing Mini-Modding, Fail RP, Godmoding, Metagaming, Powergaming, RDM, or disregarding Fear RP or Value of Life RP can lead to disciplinary action, up to a permanent ban from the server.

# GLOSSARY

## Mini-Modding

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Mini-Modding is when a player who is not staff attempts to enforce rules, investigate potential rulebreaks, or hand out consequences. If you see a rulebreak or someone is disrespecting your boundaries OOC, open a ticket. Do not try to handle it yourself. We will take care of it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Fail RP

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Fail RP is when you play in a way that breaks the setting or the RP rules. This includes breaking character with modern slang or game speak, powergaming, godmoding, ignoring Fear RP or Value of Life RP, or breaking server rules while RPing.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Godmoding

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Godmoding is when you make a character so powerful that fair RP breaks down. Your character cannot be untouchable, unkillable, or godlike. This includes never fearing death because you know you cannot be killed, claiming one-punch kills, or performing powerful magic without RP justification.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Powergaming/Powerplaying

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Powergaming is when you stack a situation so the outcome is unfairly in your favor, typically to avoid consequences. Examples include using the unstuck command to escape a hostile encounter, or inventing mechanics like mind reading or mind control without OOC consent to force others into unfair scenarios.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Metagaming

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Metagaming is when you use OOC knowledge to influence IC decisions, usually to shield your character from consequences. The following are considered meta knowledge IC:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Your character does not know what is on your HUD.</li>
      <li>You may not act IC on conversations you hear while in bat form, even though we allow hearing through walls.</li>
      <li>You must learn character names, clan affiliations, and where someone lives through IC interactions. Reading a name on a castle does not count.</li>
      <li>If a character is sneaking in rat form, you need visual confirmation before taking action. You must give an RP warning before striking a character in rat form, same as the RP notice required before PvP.</li>
      <li>Permanent clanmates know the names, locations, and identities of their clanmates even in animal form, and can tell whether a clanmate is injured or logged off.</li>
      <li>Guests joining a clan do not automatically know the names of characters they have not met IC.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Random Death Match (RDM)

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>RDM is when you attack another player without IC motivation and without declaring intent. You need a genuine RP reason to fight, and you must declare that intent before engaging. Players do not need consent to start a fight, but they do need to initiate through RP and allow a reasonable window for response. Ignoring an aggressor is not an appropriate response. See <a href="https://vardoran.com/rules#1-pvp">PvP Rules</a>.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Bleed

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Bleed is when emotions spill between IC and OOC. This is not always a bad thing. Enjoying a scene so much that you feel great OOC is bleed. The community appreciation channel is full of positive bleed. The problem is negative bleed. That is when you feel anger, resentment, or romantic attachment toward a player because of what happened in character, and then act on it. It is fine to dislike how a scene went. It is fine to want to avoid RP with someone. It is not fine to treat a player poorly OOC because their character wronged yours, or to expect OOC romance because your characters are involved.</p>
    <p>If you think bleed is affecting you or someone else, open a support ticket. See <a href="https://vardoran.com/conduct#3-icooc-awareness">IC/OOC Awareness</a> for the community standards around this. Staff is always happy to help.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Fear RP

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Fear RP is when your character acts as if their life genuinely matters. They do not know they can respawn. They do not know it is a video game. When surrounded by hostile characters, a reasonable response is fear. Dismissing danger just because you the player know there is no real risk is not Fear RP.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Value of Life RP

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Value of Life RP is when your character treats their life as if it is real. You know OOC that you can respawn. Your character does not. Every death should carry weight, whether to a player or to the world. Rushing into a boss fight and dying repeatedly without consequence is not Value of Life RP. Treating other characters' deaths as temporary is not either. Every death you deliver or receive might be the last.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## In-Character (IC)

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>IC is anything your character says or does in the game world, including IC Discord channels.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Out-of-Character (OOC)

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>OOC is anything involving you the player, not your character.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Physical Representation (PhysRep)

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>A PhysRep is when you use an in-game item to represent something the mechanics do not cover. Blood Roses for tobacco. Sculptured Wood for a magical totem. Arranging crafting stations to represent a scientific machine.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## FOIC

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Find out in character. A common phrase meaning you should learn something through RP, not through OOC knowledge.</p>
  </div>
</div>

# IN-CHARACTER TERMS

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>As a community, we have developed euphemisms for common IRL terms to maintain immersion. Use of these terms is not mandatory. You are free to come up with your own. This list is a guide to commonly-used terms in the community.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Combat Hex

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The combat debuff that appears when in combat in-game.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Fuzzy Memory

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Used to explain not being able to RP events or effects from unfinished or ongoing character stories threads in real-time.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Mosquito or Muscle Spasm

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Accidental misclicks, especially when a misclicked ability hits another player or something in their castle.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Muscles

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Keys on a keyboard or mouse clicks. "Training your muscles" means rebinding your keys.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Reconstitution

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The respawn mechanic when you have died in PvP or PvE and need to respawn at a waygate or coffin.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Sigil

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>In-game clans.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Sigil Magic

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Being in a clan is "having the sigil magic" of that clan. Being protected from friendly fire by being in a clan is "having the protection of sigil magic." Sigil magic is the magic that binds characters to a sigil.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Stars

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>A player's "stars" is their time zone. "It's late in my stars" means it is late at night for you. "If our stars align" means "if we can make our IRL schedules work." Common time zones include EST as Eastern Stars, CST as Central Stars, PST as Pacific Stars, and OST as Oceanic Stars.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Speaking Quietly/Softly

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Using text chat to RP instead of voice chat. If many players are in one place, someone may ask everyone to "speak softly," meaning everyone switches to the local in-game chat to avoid speaking over each other.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Spirits

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>IRL occurrences or obligations. "I need to be with spirits" means you are going AFK. Spirits also refers to computer issues. "Spirits have affected my ears" means audio issues. "Spirits have taken my voice" means mic issues. "Spirits of labor" means employment obligations.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Telling Time

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>We use the following terms for time. Beyond a week, we use normal IRL time units like years, decades, and centuries.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Tick: An IRL minute</li>
      <li>Turn: An IRL hour</li>
      <li>Cycle: An IRL day</li>
      <li>Days of the Week: Moons Day (Monday), Tombs Day (Tuesday), Woods Day (Wednesday), Thralls Day or Thorns Day (Thursday), Fangs Day (Friday), Sanguines Day or Saturns Day (Saturday), Suns Day (Sunday)</li>
      <li>Septurn: An IRL week</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Taking a Nap

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Relogging, usually to fix connection issues or glitches.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Throat Rip

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Executing another player that has been downed. Saying someone was throat ripped means they were killed by another character rather than by PvE.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Torpor

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Logging off or being offline. "I need to go to torpor" means you need to log off. "I'm currently in torpor but I'll be awake later" means you are offline but will be on later.</p>
  </div>
</div>
