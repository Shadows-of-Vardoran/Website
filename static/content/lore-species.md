# Vampires
The kindred undead are one of the most varied species in the world. Some are born from curses, while others are born from a virus. There are vampires who hail from long, complex sire lines-- and others who simply manifest; a bloodline all of their own. Some claim that vampires are the children of dead, fallen gods. Other claim they are a blight cursed upon the world by demonic forces.

None can say for sure the origin of all vampire-kind. The *true* source of vampirism is a secret long lost to time.

Vampires share common traits: an allergy to the sun, garlic, silver, the dark desire to consume blood, and a habit of sleeping in coffins.

## Magic-Touched Vampires
Some, though not all vampires, are touched by a particlar strain of magic. These vampires excel with their strengths, and have a few common characteristics. 

### Blood-touched
Pointier than the average vampire. Kind of though. Naturally hardy and healthy because of their connection to the source of vampire power. They tend towards being grounded and rational. Kindred magic-touched by blood are gifted with blood magic, with a secondary ease in casting storm magic. They're gifted fleshcrafters. Problems with illusion and chaos.

### Chaos-touched
Physically, chaos-touched have the highest variance of the elemental kindred. Skinny, thick, short, tall, hairy, bald, they're all over the shop. They are high energy free spirits who, while not inherently anarchists, get anxious when things remain the same for too long. Magically they have mastered the chaos and have a secondary affinity for unholy. They struggle with blood and frost.

### Unholy-touched
Favors sickly yellows, greens, and corpsely greys and whites. They tend to come in either thin almost skeletal builds or bulbous bloated bodies. They tend towards morose thinking and are very patient. They know how to wait. Still as the grave. Magically they're necromancer powerhouses and can jive pretty well with cold. Struggle with blood and storm.

### Illusion-touched
lithe and bright sometimes very unnatural colors. Often they have minor animal features and can even make incredibly minor physical changes throughout the day eye color, hair length, height. They're calculating, mischievous, and enjoy it when things aren't what they seem. Magically they are great at illusion and real solid at storm with some. Struggle with chaos and cold.

### Frost-touched
Pale, tending towards arctic blues and whites as well as very pale greens. They tend towards sharp angular features with a slightly shorter build. They are prone to jealousy, fond of creature comforts, and being logical. Magically they are as cold as ice. Struggle with illusion and chaos.

### Storm-touched
Tend towards blues, blinding whites, and the foreboding red. They tend to have springy gymnast muscle and have ever so slightly longer than normal limb-to-torso ratios. Emotional and quick to explode. They are just as quick to forget and as a result forgive. Magically they are the raging storm and know how to ride out the surge. They struggle with frost and unholy. 

# Entropic Humans

## Known Origins
During the decline of magic known as the age of entropy, a strange phenomena began to occur. Suddenly, and without warning, humans would cease to be recognized as human by their fellows. During this shift, these humans also gained access to magic beyond that which most were capable of. While for most this change occurred sometime during their adulthood, it was not unheard of for it to be passed down from parent to child as well.

In places and among peoples who already had a cooperation between humans and other supernatural beings this change was met with confusion and suspicion, but ultimately did not result in too much disruption to an individual’s life. However, in locales that had existing hostility towards supernatural beings the change could be disastrous, leading to the attack, exile, or murder of innocent people.

Lastly, and unknown to most, was what occurred when such a human died. There was a chance - slim at the start of the age, but increasing exponentially as it wore on - that at the time of death a human who had been subject to such a change would rise as an undead. A creature much like a vampire, but with a strange purple glow, who could feel in their minds the calling of others like themselves.

Given the incredibly low percentage of the human population who was affected by this phenomena, it wasn’t until after the end of the Age of Entropy that the pieces began to be put together, as knowledge about this other type of magic spread from Vardoran across the known world.

## A New Era
With an increased awareness of entropy, this phenomena became widely understood as a subspecies of human known as ‘Entropic Humans’. Acceptance and knowledge of entropic humans varies per region, with some more isolated communities still being wholly unaware of recent scholarly consensus, with other locales treating them no differently from their peers.

Current academic understanding is thus: Entropic humans are individuals that are born with an adaptation that allows them to harness entropic magic. However, this adaptation remains dormant until they have been exposed to a significant enough quantity of the magic for the proficiency to awaken. Once it has, the entropic energies that suffuse the person from that point on will cause other humans to recognize them as ‘other’, an uncanny valley effect that has no easy explanation since it is based on energy rather than physicality. The only physical difference between entropic humans and their peers is a small divot in their heart.

With persecution of entropic humans becoming less common as they are more understood, it is estimated that they make up less than 1% of the total human population, with more specific estimates being difficult to gather given the tendency of the adaptation to lay dormant within a person.

## Outside Looking In
To humans without an awakened entropic adaptation, entropic humans look exactly like they did before awakening. There is no shadowy visage or inhuman features on a literal level - the only difference is that of energy, which other humans are able to pick up on instinctually. 

Oftentimes stories will be conjured by those who experience the cognitive dissonance of seeing a friend or loved one suddenly no longer appear to be human. This is where rumors of changelings, the stealing of skin, of supernatural appearances come from. In reality these are tricks of the mind or lies as a person tries to grapple with the change they see in another. In reality, the effect is best described as an uncanny valley effect. It is an aura that an entropic human gives off that tells the one viewing them that they are somehow different, wrong, or not human.

It is this lack of any physical difference that allows education about the matter to combat much of the violence faced by entropic humans. Once the phenomena is known and understood, this sudden shift can be better rationalized and the instinctual fear can be quickly overcome. Yes, your friend may not look human anymore - but you know they are, you know the science, and that they are still themselves. This often means that even in societies which detest entropic humans on a societal level, individuals may still take actions to shield loved ones who have awakened unexpectedly.

## Death or Undeath
It is never guaranteed that a human who dies will become a rotling. For some that is the ideal, and often these humans will find themselves in the company of rotlings, perhaps even opting to be turned to remove the risk of death simply being the end of their life. Others, however, see this chance as a potential condemnation to a future of undeath. 

While there are folk practices, rumors, and rituals that claim to ensure one outcome or the other, truthfully there is no way to know if a death of an entropic human will be final or not. Often, however, for those who feared the possibility of undeath, the reality of rising as a rotling - with the comfort of the bond and the call to find your fellows - provides a port in the storm that helps them adjust to their new nature.

# ROTLINGS
Rotlings are a distinct species that emerged in the wake of the Age of Entropy. They are beings who resonate with entropic magics, and carry the distinct aura of it with them in day-to-day life. The entropic bonds between them are far weaker than they were in the prior age. Rotlings are only able to discern emotions and the general wellbeing of their fellows. And only those who are within their immediate geographical area.

During the last age, rotlings were born from the outpouring of entropic magic. Daemons and vampires and humans and fae alike were ensnared by the magic that resonated within them and were turned into rotlings.

In the current era, new rotlings may only manifest in the following ways:

1) If an entropic human perishes, they may rise from the grave as a rotling.
2) If a rotling shares blood with, bites, and drains a human, upon death, that human will rise as a rotling, not a vampire.
3) Rotling-flavored daemons; entropic daemons, manifest from the natural world, just like the daemons of the other schools of magic.

Fae and vampires cannot be resired into rotlings, nor can they become one if already undead. Entropic magic only clings in the moment of first death. It is no longer possible to transition between one species and the other.

Rotlings cannot be born, nor can they reproduce.

Rotlings are undead creatures. They are weak to sun, garlic, silver, and must consume blood and magic to survive.

Rotlings gain access to the T1 afflicted buff (purple aura) from S1.
It is no longer possible to obtain T2 Beholden, nor T3 Encased status.
Rotlings will have access to a private channel where they can send information along the rotling bond network. However, these rotlings only have access while within Vardoran. If the character leaves the country they cannot access the network.

If your character was ever a T1,2,3 contract bearer in S1, you may whitelist an existing character as a rotling. If a character from S1 did not ever receive the affliction contract, they cannot be a rotling in S2. New characters may have been afflicted elsewhere in the world, albeit off-screen for S1, or rotlings who rose during the timeskip.

Declaring a S1 character a rotling must be done at time of whitelist. You cannot do so mid-season.

Should you choose to play a rotling, know that you cannot be cured. This is simply what you are. If you are a rotling, you will remain a rotling until your true death.

## Sub Categories
Rotlings come in two distinct flavors:
*Vampiric*
*Daemonic*

***Vampiric Rotlings*** are the ones who are born from corpses, turned, bitten, or risen. They carry all the tell-tale signs of vampirism: fangs, glowing eyes, and an aversion to the vampires’ weaknesses.

***Daemonic Rotlings*** are born from an excess of entropic magic out in the world. Just like other daemons, they are required to feed on magic to survive. They inhabit corpses, and must tie themselves to a vessel to survive.

School: Entropic Magics
Nature: Entropic Daemons are curious by nature, sometimes to their detriment. They, like all rotlings, tend to trust their fellows and be weary of those outside their bonds.
Traits: Overly Curious, Nosy, Like round things
Notes: Entropic daemons are almost always a part of a cluster. They can passively feed off the entropic magic from their fellow rotlings, so it behooves them to stick with the pack.

Regardless of sub-set, all Rotlings must: drink blood, sleep in coffins, are weak to garlic, the sun, and silver. They are undead. Rotlings are rotlings *first*, rather than a vampire or daemon

## Culture
Rotlings, by nature, tend to gather in tight-knit groups. They feel each other's pain and joy. More so than any other undead species, rotlings are highly social and tend to be kind towards one another. They are prone to gathering in isolated communities– called “clusters”. 

This is, in part, due to the wave of negative reactions they garnered at the end of the last era. Many blamed them for the lack of magic, and they were met with violence as a result. Remnants of the Scions of Entropy count themselves among the Rotlings, though they are a minority. Most of them perished in the last era, or vanished on the winds.

With two centuries between the end of the last era and now, there are several burgeoning groups of rotlings pushing to be recognized as the undead equals with their peers. Why should they be punished for the actions of others from a few decades ago? Rotlings are not the majority anywhere, and have yet to gain a foothold of their own.

Rotlings have turned their faces towards this new, hopeful era. Not to turn the wheel, but to build something bigger and better and more harmonious for all.

Some of them tell tale of a longing whisper that speaks to them in the dark of night. Something within their bonds that coaxes them to wander and explore. Some worry it is the remnants of the hyphae and their wheel luring them to their doom. Others think these strange whispers are a large cluster trying to call them towards the safety they find in numbers.

Rotlings who listen to those whispers may find themselves back where all this started, back to the heart of Vardoran…

# Daemonkind

Daemons are beings formed of pure magical essence-- entities pulled from the fabric of their planes, each shaped by a distinct school of magic. A Daemon shaped by a magic school isn't necessarily bound to it for if they find a different vessel capable of withstanding multiple schools, they could grow to use more than one.

To call them demons is inaccurate; they are not born of hell or divine punishment, but rather sculpted by mortal will, ritual, or catastrophe. They are creatures of emotion and artistry, equally capable of love and cruelty, wonder and destruction. Some are tricksters, some are savants, and some are murderers. Most are all three.

Daemonic Stability is directly tied to magical saturation. Where magic is strong, Daemons flourish. Where magic is weak Daemons wither, their rate of decay entirely dependent on the Daemon's activity, magic stores and age.

To survive in a world where magic is more pervasive and easier to access, Daemons still need to inhabit the body of a once living creature to give matter to its magical form. In order to keep the corpses in a state to be used, a Daemon must consume fresh blood regularly to properly manifest their magical energy. This creates a strange adaptation that mimics vampirism, causing a deadly aversion to the sun and the craving of life energy through blood. To the untrained eye, a Daemon is indistinguishable from a Vampire.

## Subspecies of Daemons
Each Daemon is tied to one of six magical schools. Their behavior, abilities, and even appearance are shaped by this foundation.


### Blood Daemons
School: Blood Magic
Nature: Obsessive and intense
Traits: Blood-fueled strength, life siphoning, ritualistic
Notes: Often mistaken for vampires due to their lust for vitality even amongst Daemons. They see life as something to drink, wear, and shape.

### Frost Daemons
School: Ice Magic
Nature: Stoic and precise
Traits:  Want absolute control, emotional stillness, deadly patience
Notes: Considered the "elders" of Daemonkind. They do not act quickly but when they move, they are 20 steps ahead.

### Chaos Daemons
School: Chaos / Wild Magic
Nature: Unstable, excitable, volatile
Traits: Unpredictable spells, warp reality, joyful destruction
Notes: Prone to emotional outbursts. Dangerous even to allies.

### Necromantic Daemons
School: Necromancy
Nature: Calculating, bitter, morbidly curious
Traits: Corpse puppeteering, plague crafting, soul-binding
Notes Naturally drawn to graveyards and sites of great suffering. Most likely to inhabit a corpse due to their connection to the unholy.

### Illusion Daemons
School: Illusion
Nature: Mysterious, performative, manipulative
Traits: Create false realities, commit identity theft
Notes: Often artists or dramatists. Will fabricate entire personas and believe them.

### Storm Daemons
School: Storm / Lightning Magic
Nature: Proud, loud, wrathful
Traits: Thunderous magic, atmospheric control, understanding of Nature
Notes; Often mistaken for ''angry clouds''. Their tempers shape the weather around them but refuse to harm trees.

## On Decay and Dependency
Daemons are not immortal. Without access to magic-- through ley lines, blood, storms, ritual, or war-- they begin to decay:

*Their forms become unstable or fragment.
*Their minds become lost.
*They wither, becoming hungry spirits or sleeping things in deep places.

To prevent this, Daemons may bind themselves to the dead, especially fallen mages or magically potent corpses. 

# Faekind
## Origins of the Fae
Before the collapse of magic, long before the current world order and the rise of technocrats and fractured republics, there were the Ancient Vampires-- eldritch, proud, and cursed beings who stalked the night. Yet over time, some among them began to resent their own limitations.

They could not bask in sunlight.
They could not touch silver.
They could not cross running water.
They were enslaved to bloodlust and eternal decay.

To transcend their cursed bodies, a sect of vampires—obsessed with perfection and endless indulgence—abandoned undeath as they knew it. Channelling the height of magical science and fuelled by centuries of knowledge and blood ritual, they committed the ultimate act of magical ability and ambition: they created their own realm.

This new domain, known now as Thyl Myrith, was a perfect illusion. A sandbox reality where magic bent to one's will and boredom was the only true adversary. There, these ancient vampires reshaped themselves, generation after generation. Physical traits softened. Fangs dulled. Skin took on color. The ''sun'' above their conjured sky no longer seared their flesh. They could walk, breathe, taste, and laugh without hunger gnawing at their soul.

They became Fae.

The early euphoria of evolution gave way to a greater curse: eternal boredom. Without natural death, without want or consequence, Faekind found themselves plagued by apathy. They became tricksters and sadists, finding entertainment only in manipulation, illusion, and cruelty simply because nothing else was interesting. Their influence on the mortal world with their illusions made them become known as ''Creative Devils'' amongst villagers and their victims. Due to their evolution, they have slight physiological differences with their Vampiric predecessors; their fangs have remained dulled after their time in Thyl Myrith with their skin colour slightly less pale. A sub-species of Vampire, they know no bound in their hunger for entertainment. 


## Aftermath of the Collapse
As the Collapse of Magic began rippling through all planes, Thyl Myrith-- being a realm founded entirely on magical sustainment, began to rot from the inside. Landscapes once reshaped at whim turned stiff, unchanging, lifeless. The illusion cracked.

And for the first time in countless centuries, Faekind remembered fear.

For what is a sandbox when the sand turns to ash?

Desperate to avoid extinction, Faekind migrated en-masse into the material world they once left behind. There, they were reminded of every weakness they had cast off.

Sunlight burned them.
Silver harmed them.
The curse of blood, though distant, lingered.
Worse still, they could die, truly and permanently.
And nothing scares a Fae like the end of amusement.

## Two Hundred Years After Thyl Myrith

Two centuries have passed since Thyl Myrith rotted away. It was not destroyed—simply undone. Its sky, its soil, its memory—all erased without trace into the entropic wheel that once plagued the world. Few remember that it ever was, and even they are unsure if they remember correctly.
Those who once called themselves Fae have outlasted belief itself.
They are quieter now, smaller, content to pass unseen.
Most who meet one mistake them for what they always feared becoming:
vampires.
And perhaps they are.

## The Fading Line
The distinction between Faekind and the vampires they once despised has nearly disappeared. Their illusions have grown faint, subtle things—more habit than magic. A shimmer in the air when they smile. A shadow that lingers a heartbeat too long. Nothing remarkable.
They drink blood when they must, though it sickens them. They hide from the sun for it still burns them just as fiercely. They no longer float above the curse—they simply manage it, the way mortals manage hunger and time.
They have, at last, learned to live as part of the world instead of apart from it.

## The Quieting
The Fae call the death of their kind the Quieting. It does not mark the end of a life—it marks acceptance. A Fae who quiets has ceased chasing illusions. They no longer try to recreate Thyl Myrith in the air, or in their own reflection. They stop searching for the perfection they once believed they deserved.
In its place grows something strange: contentment.
They work, they travel, they learn mortal crafts.
A few have even taken companions.
Where once they mocked humanity, now they envy it.
Where once they fed on despair, now they survive on patience.

## The World’s Forgetting
The modern age no longer cares for stories of the Fae. Many call them “a variant strain,” scholars dismiss them as exaggerated vampires. No one fears them. No one worships them.
And perhaps that is what saves them.
The Fae no longer seek to stand apart. They fade comfortably into the background noise of cities, forgotten nobility and quiet poets, living on memory and moonlight. They are no longer the architects of illusion—they are its quiet inheritors, blending seamlessly with what remains of magic in the world.

## The Glimmer
Sometimes, though, on the rarest nights, one of them will dream of color—brilliant, impossible hues from a place that no longer exists. Then it fades, leaving only stillness.
But for that moment, it is enough.
Perhaps there is hope, perhaps it is nothing more than delusion. One thing is certain, Thyl Myrith is gone, the magics which held it together have been consumed by rot.
And so Faekind continue, unremarkable and unfeared,
neither monsters nor miracles—just another kind of lost people,
living quietly at the edge of a world that no longer dreams.

## IMPORTANT NOTE
All Fae must have existed in this world for at least 200 years. Thyl Mirith, their home realm, has been gone for that long, so please include what they have been up to for the last 200 years in your whitelist!!!!
