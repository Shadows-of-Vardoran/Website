<h1 id="vardoran" class="leading-none">Vardoran</h1>
*A Kingless Kingdom, a Forgotten Backwater*

## Historical Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Vardoran has seen kingdoms and empires rise and fall, and human settlement has always found its way back to its fertile valleys. Conquerors have come and gone, but the small folk of Vardoran have always endured.</p>
    <p>It was once a part of Nosterra, an ancient empire that considered all of the western continent a part of its expanse. After Nosterra's fall in 1-AF, Vardoran was simply abandoned as a colony, and never reabsorbed into any of its neighboring nations. Today, it is considered somewhere between a region and a city-state. Vardoran does not have a widespread government, nor laws. It still has a sizable population who call it home and consider themselves Vardoranians despite their lack of international recognition.</p>
    <p>Vardoran is a region of several small valleys hidden away in the Hallowed Mountains. These low valleys have large swaths of fertile farmland and lush forests. Vardoran is home to several small micro-cultures, and the communities therein vary vastly from region to region.</p>
    <p>Much of the world did not know Vardoran even existed until Dracula began his campaign of conquest in 1264 AF. It is under considerable scrutiny in the current year, as some blame the region for magic's existence. Scholars, such as the Archivum, have flocked en masse to Vardoran as a result.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Racial and Magical Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">

    <p>In Vardoran, vampires prospered before Dracula's rise, and they continue to survive despite the light magic of Luminance. Vampires, able to control all forms of magic, have continued to thrive in Vardoran's political dysfunction. In recent years, the Werewolves of the land too have begun to band together in a communal refuge from their affliction.</p>
    <p>Without a formal government able to crack down on any single group, Vardoran is an attractive home for many Mytts and Talam as well. Perhaps though, Vardoran is a less attractive place for humans, whose frail mortality is pushed to its limits by the hardships of the land.</p>

## Subfactions of Vardoran

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">

    <p>Within the stateless land of Vardoran, different groups have decided to take power into their own hands. The Cult of the Damned resurrect the long dead and their empire. The Legion of Noctum kidnap citizens and conjure monsters for the return of their fallen king. The Church of Luminance tries to hold onto their dominance of the land and struggle with the newfound chaos brought by magic's widespread arrival. The Dunley Militia do what they can to protect the people amidst desertion and pressure from all sides. Deserters from the Militia and the Light band together as bandits, keen to make a profit in the chaos. Transcendum viciously assert their occupation of Gloomrot and The Venomblades secure their borders in Oakveil.</p>
    <p>All of Vardoran is in a state of unsteady chaos, torn in every direction and vulnerable to the influence of the creatures of the night.</p>

<h1 id="aetheria-the-land-of-the-air-and-stars" class="leading-none">Aetheria, the Land of the Air and Stars</h1>
<p class="italic text-tprimary-400">(ay-thee-ree-uh)<br>Founded in 1268</p>

## Historical Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Aetheria. The Land of the Air and Stars. It is a collection of three large islands and eight small ones. It is said that Aetheria is a mountain range that rose from the sea with the emergence of magic. Their shores are sharp, and their peaks high above the clouds. The land is difficult to navigate, and thus innately safe from outsiders.</p>
    <p>Following the emergence of magic in 1268-AF, Talam gathered around the three islands of Aetheria: Solaria, Twilaria, and Lunaria, and formally founded their nation. Aetheria rests atop a multi-pronged magical hotspot. A massive wellspring of natural magics, it acts as a bastion against the magical realm, constantly pushing to balance the surge of magic that occurred. It has made magic and vegetation plentiful, despite the harsh climate of the tall mountains.</p>
    <p>Aetherians are dedicated to the study of magic. The Talam wish to gather more of their people and to understand the strange world in which they now find themselves. The study of magic is vital, and the passion of all Aetherians.</p>
    <p>Their nation is a young one, ruled over by the founding Talam who found the islands and declared them home. Aetherians refer to this collective as The Eleven. They serve as a judicial and logistical ruler, deciding where paths will be built and bridges will be implemented. The Eleven are a mix of the three Circles, not favoring one over either of the others.</p>
    <p>The Eleven are spiritual leaders, trusted elders, and beloved members of their communities. They are listened to when they speak, venerated by their peers for finding the beautiful mountains that Aetherians call home. All eleven of them are Talam.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Cultural Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Each island is centralized around the Three Circles: Talaghria on Solaria, Talagaela on Twilaria, and Talafaesga on Lunaira. However, travel between the three is encouraged and very common. The mountains are very close together, so much so that the three islands can be walked between during low tide.</p>
    <p>Large bridges connect the mountains, each made from cables of metal and durable planks of wood. Pillars have been built from the sea to act as foundations for the bridges. They are a modern architectural marvel, able to withstand high winds and the spray of the sea.</p>
    <p>Known for its exports of rare silks, gems, jewelry, and things not of this realm, Aetheria is a land where the impossible is made real, a place of indescribable beauty and grace, of islands that pierce the sky, wreathed in mist and clouds. Its people find joy in the elegant simplicity of a life seeped in magic.</p>
    <p>Talam culture is synonymous with Aetherian culture. They have a healthy respect for magic and the natural world, living side by side with nature. Balance between the living and the natural world is a core tenant of their beliefs. As is worship of the Sun.</p>
    <p>Invention that relies on burning machines and bottled lightning is not prized. It is forbidden. For to challenge the power of magic's rule is to challenge the way of the world itself. The balance and natural order is coveted. Those who step outside the lines are swiftly banished from Aetheria's peaks.</p>
    <p>Aetherians eat from carefully maintained terrace farms and the generous fresh water springs the mountains provide. It is a place of beauty, with abundant waterfalls and curious collections of floating pebbles that circle the highest parts of the mountains. Some Aetherians believe that, should they fully grasp the depths of magic, someday they, too, shall fly.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<h1 id="ajania" class="leading-none">Ajania</h1>
<p class="italic text-tprimary-400">(uh-hay-knee-uh)<br>Founded: 200 years before the fall of Nosterra</p>

## Historical Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Two hundred years before the fall of Nosterra, Ajania's capital was founded as a distant silver mining colony for Nosterra. A myriad of legionnaires settled the Gallant Mountains to secure valuable silver, copper, and lead for the Empire. From early years, plumes of smoke billowed up from the valleys and poisoned the clouds. Expended quarries and open-pit mines flooded into poisoned lakes. Workers collapsed after inhaling poisonous fumes from deep within the caves. The people of Ajania learned and adapted. They researched the causes of these disasters and made changes to prevent the worst impacts of their extraction. They developed treatments and medicines for the poisons their country was founded to work with, and they used their enormous stockpiles of silver to leverage surrounding nations to trade them necessary resources.</p>
    <p>Ajania quickly grew to become one of the world's wealthiest and best defended countries. It persisted after the fall of Nosterra and claims to still be the rightful heir-state to that empire. For centuries, Ajania ruled the region as the de-facto power, influencing other countries around it.</p>
    <p>Eventually though, seven hundred years ago, silver lost favor as the currency for trade. Gold replaced it and Ajania's influence quickly declined. Lead and copper became more valuable exports and silver was no longer nearly as valuable. During this era, silver became an excess material that was used wherever it could find use. Usually it was simply used as part of construction; ore-bearing stone was cut into bricks as much as any other masonry. Many of these buildings still stand today.</p>
    <p>Three hundred years ago, silver's value again skyrocketed. The rise of the first vampires and the introduction of magic into the world led to a practical and essential use for silver: the protection of humanity. With an increase of leisure that came with this wealth, Ajanians turned to expressions of their creativity and redoublings into their health. Doctors came from around the world to study in Ajanian hospitals. Skilled stone carvers received patronage to carve great sculptures into the peaks of the mountains surrounding the capital city, which were long since depleted of ore. In these past centuries, Ajania's importance and influence has turned it into a cultural and economic hub not just for the region, but for the world.</p>
    <p>Now, with magic and monsters redoubling their effect on the world, Ajania finds itself a nation wealthy beyond words. Its people would be free to celebrate and live leisurely lives, if not for the recent troubling developments. Child and infant mortality and illness are on the rise. Inexplicably, some 15% of newborns in the country are facing severe illness. Those who survive tend to be sickly into their early childhood. Not enough time has passed yet to know for certain if they can eventually recover. If the cause of this illness is not found and solved, Ajania, a kingdom that stood for over a millennium, will collapse within a few short generations, and with it, the world's supply of silver.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Cultural Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Centuries of prosperity have given the people of Ajania a sense that they are "chosen." Fated to live in prosperity eternally within the mountains, isolated from the world at large. They have solved their historic problems with engineering and policy and generally have figured out their niche within the world. The rise of vampires, and now magic, has propelled them into incredible wealth, divided amongst the people. It is not an even divide, but there is so much excess wealth and food that most adults do not have any wants. While a minority of the population handles the governance, logistics, and mineral extraction, the majority are free to pursue scholarship, arts, and cultural achievements.</p>
    <p>So long as they can trade with surrounding nations for food and essentials, their limitless supplies of silver and defensible terrain lets them strive for the heavens in science and arts. They are, however, still quite superstitious. Ajanian culture has stayed consistent for over 1400 years.</p>
    <p>However, the child mortality crisis has become the defining concern of the decade. Ten years ago it began, and it has only gotten worse since. This generation alive in Ajania may very well be the country's last. Its culture is facing this existential threat and the people are divided with hundreds of theories and ideas. Causing significant miscommunication and cultural worry. The citizenry really does have so much time on their hands, so much knowledge, so much skill, but so little ability to save themselves.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Important and Notable Landmarks/Places

#### The Sculpted Peaks

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>These mountaintops surround Ajania's capital. They were the first areas depleted of their silver in the ancient past, so skilled engineers and artists took centuries to carve them into beautiful sculptures on an unprecedented scale. The sculptures vary in size and age, but the newest and largest, completed last year, is The Phoenix, a mythical bird with silver and gold traced into its feathers, carved in the image of a peacock. Other notable peaks are Hecatonkeres: a peak carved into a hundred busts, representing the original soldiers who settled Ajania. The faces are actually modeled after the friends of the mason who carved it. Her descendants have maintained it for three hundred years.</p>
  </div>
</div>

#### Myriad Mirrors

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>A public works project turned art installation. It was incredibly costly to have giant planes of glass shaped into lenses abroad and transported carefully through mountain tunnels. Only once they were put into place with the silver mirrors carefully polished did the capital become capable of enjoying light during the nights. Moon and star light is focused onto mirrors and guided above the city to be split into prisms that work as street lights. The natural lights of night catch the silver in the brickwork and the whole city glows every evening.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Those who follow the old ways keep to sky burials hoping for many corvids to show up as they are believed to carry souls on their wings to where they must go. Lightning is the Storm Speaker's voice and snow the Frost Father's influence thus storms of all kinds are revered as important events. Folghan is attributed for many great feats like raising mountains with strikes of his hammer upon pure metals but none know how true they really are.</p>

The Parlor meets within the Crimson Hall, a large room built in the outermost area of the Inner Palace, and is the only part of the Inner Palace that is accessible to those who do not live within it.</p>
    <p>The High Table, which is located behind a screen, is made up of ten members, all of whom are believed to be second and third generation vampires of the Five Descendants. Some vampires who are very wealthy, have large clans, or are uniquely powerful, may find themselves at the Low Table. The Low Table has 80 members. These members vote on matters that are presented to the Parlor. Should they tie, the High Table discusses the matter and makes the final decision.</p>
    <p>Getting an invitation to the Low Table is a great honor. Clans engage in complicated political games to try and garner their seat. It is a source of betrayal, murder, and strife.</p>

The Congregation is made up of vampires who lack power, are of small clans, or have little to no relation to the Progenitor. They do not get a vote, nor can they speak during Parlor gatherings, but they may bring written matters for the Parlor to discuss if they obtain the signatures of fifty clans. Members of the Congregation sit in long rows on bench seats below the Low Table.</p>

    <p>Sul'uun has a long, complicated history. A unification of tribes in 12-AF founded their kingdom in the heart of the forest of Uln. They enjoyed rapid expansion, plentiful harvests, and abundant rivers. In just a single century the Kingdom of Sul'uun became the largest country in the world.</p>
    <p>At their onset, they worshiped a god of the sun. It was he who ensured their harvests were plentiful and their shores safe. It was to him that they pledged their loyalties. People of Sul'uun refer to him as the Great Sun, or the Holy Sun.</p>

The once plentiful forests are naught but a barren desert. Sand, red and burning, stretches as far as the eyes can see. Nothing grows in Sul'uun. The soil is unfit for life.</p>

## Cultural Overview

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-rose-800/60 to-rose-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-rose-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Sul'uun is a nation focused on survival. While there is some desert vegetation they can eat, they rely largely on hunting wryms, large sand-dwelling beasts that dwell within the earth, for food. They drink the creature's blood and viscera instead of water. They eat the guts and fashion the hard shells into tools.</p>
    <p>Sul'uun imports large amounts of dirt and water to grow what food they can. Food is grown in large, hanging baskets. Should those baskets ever touch the ground the soil becomes corrupted by the Sun God's Curse and turns into red sand. Baskets of soil are carried miles across the desert, into the subterranean cities or hilltop settlements. To drop vital soil and tarnish it is a crime punishable by death.</p>
    <p>Sul'uunians have adopted several features of the great wyrms that they are forced to consume. Many have red or yellow eyes, and their skin has taken on a reddish or pink hue. They are a culture that believes in punishment, hard work, and a hearty heaping of a fear for the divine.</p>
    <p>"It is by the Mercy of the Moon that we survive. It is in the Curse of the Sun that we burn."</p>
    <p>The Empire of Sul'uun is unique in its worship, as they have a dual faith serving both the Sun and the Moon, the faith of Luntoll. There are ceremonies performed every day and every night to assuage the Sun's ire, and try to stay in the Moon's good graces. Common rituals are nightly prayer, group meditations, and collective dance.</p>
    <p>The people of Sul'uun believe that the union of Lythe and Sul'uun represented a marriage between the Sun and the Moon. The Sun is referred to with masculine pronouns, while the Moon is distinctly feminine. Weddings are held at sunset or sunrise, when the divine couple shares the sky. Weddings held during the day or night are considered to be doomed at their onset.</p>
    <p>To be caught worshipping another deity within Sul'uun is a crime. They believe that the Sun would take it as an insult, and heretics are executed immediately to ensure that the Sun does not punish their neighbors for their foolish transgressions.</p>

    <p>Welronz is a nation that values hard work and dedication. Farmers and ranchers are the lifeblood of their communities. Entire towns pop up around rich cattle ranges and most people in their direct vicinity all work on the same swath of land. There are people to work the leather, others to grow the wheat, and many more to raise the cattle until it is time for slaughter. The land itself is held by a very small portion of the population, who are both the richest in the nation and members of the ruling class.</p>
    <p>Due to most cities being centralized around single-ownership swaths of lands, there is a sense of home and pride in their small communities, as well as a rivalry between those who are close enough to cross paths. It is very common for marriages to be arranged by the community leaders; young people are sent great distances across Welronz to secure trade deals and to ensure steady populations nation-wide.</p>
    <p>Welronz is one of the largest countries in the world, but it has one of the smallest populations. Over-farming and poor agricultural practices are blamed for the One Hundred Year Drought, and as a result Welronzians are hyper-vigilant of their population. They do not want to sacrifice crop rotation, as it is believed that it will lead to another continent-wide disaster.</p>
    <p>Farming and agriculture in general are considered the great cornerstones of Welronz. Agriculturalists and knowledgeable farmers are among their most lauded historical figures.</p>
    <p>Welronz is predominantly atheist, as any magic, even that of the divine, is met with distrust and hatred.</p>

Some believe it to be nothing more than fable, while others claim to have seen it and smelt its foul breath while sailing across the lake. The locals refer to this mysterious creature as the "Deepwater Serpent", and much of the local folklore centers around sightings of the creature.</p>
    <p>Nobody knows for sure how deep the lake is. None have successfully found the bottom. Divers die several times a year, and locals warn that those who are lost to the depths are unlikely to ever be recovered.</p>
    <p>The trees around Deepwater Lake are some of the largest in the world. There are root systems that feed directly into the lake itself. Standing on the edge and looking out over clear water gives the illusion of thousands of fingers reaching out from the lake's edge because of the roots that press in from all sides. Local fishermen use these roots as a way to fasten their nets and fish traps under water.</p>

#### Lythe Rainforest

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gray-800/60 to-gray-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gray-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <div style="float: right; margin: 0 0 1rem 1rem; max-width: 400px; width: 100%;">
      <img src="/assets/lythe_rainforest.png" alt="Lythe Rainforest">
    </div>
    <p>Located at the heart of the eastern continent, the Lythe Rainforest is home to dense trees, constant rain, and ferocious wildlife. There are frogs bigger than men, armed with toxins and acidic spit. There are alligators that are as long as two oxen. With several types of jungle cats that seem to enjoy picking off the humans to dare to venture into the canopies, the Lythe Rainforest is not a place for the ill-prepared nor the faint of heart.</p>
    <p>Those who brave it for valuable pelts, scales, and herbs are likely to travel up and down the various rivers that sprawl through the region. Moving by canoe or rowboat is common. However, large groups that make too much noise are sunk by the Lythe's flying piranha.</p>
    <p>While the region was once a part of the Sul'uun Empire, and the Kingdom of Lythe prior to that, any settlements within it were abandoned after the Empire's rise and subsequent curse in 334 AF. In the centuries since, the rainforest only grows more inhospitable as the roads and safehavens that once existed within it have been slowly but surely reclaimed by nature.</p>

#### Forest of Uln

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gray-800/60 to-gray-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gray-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Located at the northernmost edge of Sul'uun, the Forest of Uln is all that remains of the great forest that once covered the bulk of the eastern continent. Following the Sun's Curse in 334 AF, Sul'uunites abandoned the area a little over a century later, superstition driving them to believe that remaining in the forest would only ensure its destruction.</p>
    <p>Despite lacking a Sul'uunian presence within the forest, the red sands of the desert continue to encroach on the forest to this day. Trees on its southern edge have been dyed red by the sands, and are swallowed by the desert once depleted of water.</p>
    <p>The Forest of Uln is primarily composed of deciduous trees, which collectively turn yellow and gold in the fall. This time is referred to as the "golden storm", as the gold leaves fall in a short time period and are blown south all over Sul'uun. Catching more than one leaf at the same time between thumb and forefinger on a single hand is said to bring good luck for the remainder of the year.</p>

#### Whispering Peaks

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gray-800/60 to-gray-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gray-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Marking the tallest point and the northern edge of the eastern continent, the Whispering Peaks are known for their sheer inclines and deadly rockslides. The region consists of shale and loose rock, as well as several small volcanoes.</p>
    <p>The sound of distant eruptions and winds moving through the rocky mountains creates an odd whispering sound on the wind. It is from this strange phenomenon that the region gets its name.</p>
    <p>Locals and those who frequent the area believe that following any whispers is sure to lead one to their doom. It is a difficult mountain range to traverse, not only due to the sheer cliffs and unstable rocks, but also because of the near-constant earthquakes, landslides, and the smoke that settles into its valleys from the volcanoes in the area.</p>

#### The World's Teeth

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gray-800/60 to-gray-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gray-900/30"></div>

      <img src="/assets/the_worlds_teeth.png" alt="The World's Teeth">

    <p>A long island composed exclusively of volcanoes, the World's Teeth are a series of deadly peaks that were carved out in the sea directly off the coast of Hafjallheim. Smoke columns from the Teeth's volcanoes erupt year-round, and sometimes travel as far south as Norus.</p>
    <p>The stretch of sea between the Teeth and Hafjallheim is one of the most dangerous in the world. Rock, smoke, deadly gas, and pyroclastic materials rain constantly into the sea, making fish scarce and travel deadly.</p>
    <p>There is an ancient and respected practice of reading the smoke columns for oracles. The Frost Maiden of Hafjallheim is believed to be the first person to do so, and her legacy has made the Teeth's eruptions into an important cultural event.</p>
