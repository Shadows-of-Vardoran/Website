<!-- section:blood-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-red-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-red-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Only vampires can use blood magic.</span></div>

Blood magic is rooted in life force, the vital energy that animates living things. It treats that energy as a transferable resource to be drawn, redirected, and spent. It is the oldest school of magic, woven into the nature of vampirism itself, and it belongs, fundamentally, to the undead.

Its themes are life pushed past its natural limits. Sustenance and hunger. The body as both tool and vessel. Preservation, corruption, and the fine line between healing and harm. Every working comes at someone's expense. It is intimate, parasitic, and extraordinarily powerful in the right hands.

All vampires can channel blood magic instinctively. It is woven into their nature rather than learned, and basic applications come without instruction. However, instinct is not mastery. Advanced applications such as fleshcrafting, precise biological manipulation, and complex ritual work require dedicated study and practice like any other school of magic.

<div class="p-4 rounded bg-background-800/60 border border-red-800/60 my-4">
  <span class="text-warm font-bold">Having blood magic innately is not the same as having the blood magic specialty.</span> All vampires can channel blood magic in combat and use it for basic purposes without a specialty. Notable use of blood magic in an RP context beyond standard in-game use does require the specialty. Innate ability and specialized skill are separate things.
</div>

## What You Can Do

- Transfer life force from a living source to fuel healing, sustain the caster, or power a working.
- Heal injuries by redirecting vitality into damaged tissue (with consent).
- Modify and reshape living flesh and biological systems with sufficient skill.
- Alter a living body in ways that push it beyond its natural state (RP only, no mechanical effects).
- Sustain life in conditions that would otherwise be fatal, through magical life support.
- Store life force in physical mediums and route it through objects and rituals, even when a mortal is handling it.

## What You Cannot Do

- Blood magic cannot generate life force from nothing. The conservation rule is absolute. What is spent must come from somewhere, and without a living source, there is nothing to work with.
- Blood magic cannot be channeled directly by a mortal. The barrier is structural, not a matter of training or talent. A mortal who attempts it suffers damage.
- Blood magic cannot resurrect the dead. Life force that has left a body cannot be forced back into it.

<!-- section:unholy-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-green-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-green-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Only vampires and humans can use unholy magic.</span></div>

Unholy magic is the school of will and intent. Where other schools manipulate forces or substances, unholy magic imposes the practitioner's purpose directly onto magical energy, saturating it with their intent and turning it into a carrier for what they want. The energy itself is irrelevant. The intent is everything. Its themes are willpower, agency, the persistence of purpose, and the residue that mortal minds leave behind in the world after death.

Graveyards, battlefields, places of worship, anywhere that intense mortal will has been exercised over time, these locations accumulate will-saturated magical energy that practitioners can draw on. The dead leave something behind too, not souls, but fragments of who they were, echoes of a mind that once had will and purpose, lingering in magically saturated ground and waiting to be compelled to speak, at least for a time.

## What You Can Do

- Animate dead matter by imprinting it with magical energy saturated with basic instructions and intent.
- Compel the mental fragments of the dead to manifest and interact, drawing on the residual imprint of their will and memory.
- Draw on environmentally accumulated will saturation in locations where mortal intent has soaked into the ground over time.
- Sustain workings through stored will-saturated magical energy, allowing effects to persist beyond active channeling.

## What You Cannot Do

- Unholy magic cannot control the living. An active will resists and adapts faster than any finite intent can suppress it.
- Unholy magic cannot conjure physical objects or create something from nothing. It operates on what already exists.
- Unholy magic cannot resurrect the dead. Fragments are residual imprints of will, not the person. Compelling one to speak is not bringing them back.
- Compelling a fragment consumes it. Extended interrogation uses up what remains. Once it is gone, it cannot be contacted again.

<!-- section:storm-magic -->
Storm magic is the school of concentrated energy. It uses magical energy to manipulate mundane energy or magical energy, gathering and focusing it against its natural tendency to disperse. Its themes are concentration, accumulation, intensity, and an intuitive attunement to how magical energy flows and pools in the environment.

Storm magic is not lightning. It is not elemental in nature. Magical energy can take a bolt-like form because it is the purest way raw energy can physically express itself in the world, not because it shares anything fundamental with mundane electricity. Storm practitioners develop a natural sense for energy movement, sensing where it is concentrated, depleted, or shifting. This tends to make them exceptional at rituals and artificing, where understanding how magical energy flows through a working is as important as the working itself.

## What You Can Do

- Concentrate and direct raw magical energy for destructive or constructive purposes.
- Sense and analyze magical energy flow in the environment.
- Detect latent enchantments and map energy patterns in magical structures.
- Store concentrated magical energy in physical mediums such as topaz for later use.
- Push toward converting magical energy to mundane energy or the reverse, though this is genuinely unnatural and the consequences are unpredictable and potentially catastrophic.

## What You Cannot Do

- Storm magic cannot create energy from nothing. It works with what already exists, concentrating what is present rather than generating new energy.
- Storm magic cannot produce its effects without a source to draw from. Without energy present, there is nothing to concentrate.
- Storm magic cannot sustain concentrated energy indefinitely without effort or a storage medium. The world constantly pushes back toward equilibrium.

## Features

- Access to storm magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:frost-magic -->
Frost magic is the school of energy depletion. It uses magical energy to manipulate mundane energy or magical energy, removing it from a target or area and causing cold and ice as the physical consequence of that removal. Frost practitioners do not create elemental cold. They remove energy, which decreases heat. Its themes are depletion, deficit, the world's constant push toward equilibrium, and the structural knowledge gained through breaking things.

Every frost effect is a temporary state of imbalance that the world is constantly trying to correct, requiring continuous channeling to maintain. Magical energy fuels the removal process, spending itself to pull mundane energy out of a space the way a pump spends effort to move water. Practitioners can construct physical structures out of ice by removing energy from ambient moisture, causing spontaneous condensation and freezing through shaped areas of low energy. Ice pillars, barriers, and platforms are byproducts of that deficit, not acts of creation. Frost practitioners can also target magical energy directly, allowing them to influence flow through wards and magical constructs, though this is more demanding and less predictable than working with mundane energy.

## What You Can Do

- Remove mundane energy from a target or area to produce cold and ice as a physical consequence.
- Shape areas of low energy to produce ice structures such as barriers, platforms, or supports.
- Target magical energy in wards and constructs to disrupt or analyze their structure.
- Store energy vacuums in sapphires for later deployment without active channeling.

## What You Cannot Do

- Frost magic cannot create energy or add energy to a system. It only removes.
- Frost magic cannot produce permanent effects without a storage medium. Every effect requires ongoing channeling or it collapses as the world equalizes.
- Frost magic cannot create or repair anything directly. Ice structures are consequences of energy removal, not acts of creation.

## Features

- Access to frost magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:illusion-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-teal-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-teal-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Incompatible with Elemental and Druidic magic.</span></div>

Illusion magic is the school of creation through comprehension. It manifests anything the practitioner truly understands, and the limit is never power but always the depth of that understanding. Its themes are creation, comprehension, the relationship between knowledge and reality, and the impermanence of what any mortal mind can truly know.

Illusions are not phantoms or tricks of perception. They are tangible and real while they exist. An illusory sword can cut. An illusory shield can block. But no mortal practitioner can fully comprehend anything at the level reality itself does, which means every creation is incomplete, a reflection of what the practitioner imagines something to be rather than what it truly is. The moment concentration breaks, the creation dissolves. Imperfections are not a sign of poor skill. They are the nature of the school. What one imagines something to be is never exactly what it is, and that gap is always visible in the work.

## What You Can Do

- Manifest temporary tangible objects, structures, or effects from comprehension.
- Create unique effects drawn from imagination and imposed on a target.
- Produce physical consequences through created things, damage from an illusory blade, shelter from an illusory wall.
- Create increasingly accurate and complex manifestations through deeper study and understanding of what you are creating.

## What You Cannot Do

- Illusion magic cannot create permanently. All manifestations dissolve when concentration breaks or the practitioner stops channeling.
- Illusion magic cannot destroy directly. Destruction is a side effect of what is created, not something the magic itself does.
- Illusion magic cannot control or influence the will and minds of others directly.
- Imperfect understanding produces imperfect results. A creation is only ever as complete as the practitioner's comprehension of it.

## Features

- Access to illusion magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:chaos-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-purple-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-purple-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Incompatible with Elemental and Druidic magic.</span></div>

Chaos magic is the school of unmaking. Its operative effect is erasure. Things subject to chaos magic stop existing entirely, with no residue or remnant left behind. Its themes are destruction, the removal of what exists, the discipline of precision, and the weight of wielding something that cannot be undone.

Chaos fire is not chemical fire. It is a visual artifact of something being removed from reality. It is not hot. It does not burn in the conventional sense. It does not spread to adjacent material the way normal fire does. It is simply what erasure looks like.

At its furthest reaches, chaos magic can push beyond physical matter into something more dangerous, potentially unmaking concepts, ideas, or other things that exist without having a physical form. This is not a school that forgives a wandering mind. An unfocused practitioner does not simply fail. They erase the wrong thing. This is a school that carries real weight, and we will treat it accordingly.

## What You Can Do

- Erase physical matter, objects, and structures entirely with no residue.
- Destroy magical workings, dismantle wards, and destabilize enchantments.
- Push toward unmaking concepts or non-physical things at the extreme upper limit of the school.

## What You Cannot Do

- Chaos magic cannot create, construct, repair, or add anything to the world. It only removes.
- Chaos magic cannot reverse what it has done. Erasure is permanent.
- Chaos magic cannot function without extraordinary mental discipline. An unfocused working does not fail safely. It erases what it touches rather than what was intended.

## Features

- Access to chaos magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:light-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-yellow-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-yellow-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Incompatible with Elemental and Druidic magic.</span></div>

Light magic is the school of truth. It reveals and restores the true state of things, and those two functions are the same thing seen from different angles. To reveal what is true is already a partial restoration, because concealment and corruption are departures from truth. Pushing something back toward what it truly is and exposing what it truly is are the same motion. Its themes are truth, revelation, restoration, clarity, and the cost of self-deception.

Light magic healing does not impose a new arrangement on a wounded body. It pushes the body back toward what it naturally should be, restoring it toward its true state. The school can also be used offensively, its physical manifestation deployed as a weapon like any other magical school, but that is a product of war rather than the nature of the magic itself. A practitioner's connection to the school is directly tied to their own relationship with truth. Those who live in self-deception, who hide who they are from themselves, will find the school resistant and difficult. No mortal can live in a state of perfect truth, and that natural limitation is the school's ceiling.

## What You Can Do

- Reveal the true state of things, piercing concealment, illusion, and disguise (with consent).
- Restore things toward their natural true state, including healing injuries by pushing the body back toward what it should be (with consent).
- Dissolve shadow magic corruption and other effects that have pulled something away from its true nature.
- Deploy light magic offensively as a physical manifestation, though this runs against the school's fundamental character.

## What You Cannot Do

- Light magic cannot restore death. The true state of a living thing does not persist after death in a form the school can act on.
- Light magic cannot compel truth from a person or influence their will. It acts on the state of things, not the agency of people.
- Light magic cannot restore what no longer exists. If the true state has been destroyed rather than concealed or corrupted, there is nothing to push back toward.

## Features

- Access to light magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:shadow-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-gray-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-gray-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Incompatible with Elemental and Druidic magic.</span></div>

Shadow magic is the school of concealment and corruption. It hides what something truly is and warps it away from its true state, and those two functions are the same thing seen from different angles, just as revealing and restoring are their opposite. Its themes are concealment, corruption, decay, secrets, and the willingness to dwell in what is hidden.

Shadow magic does not create anything new. It acts entirely on what already exists, obscuring true nature or pulling it away from its original state. At its most interesting it becomes a tool of discovery, warping what exists into states the practitioner could never have imagined, finding what lies on the other side of corruption rather than aiming for a predetermined outcome. A practitioner's connection to the school is tied to their comfort with the hidden and obscured. Those who demand clarity and insist on seeing things as they truly are will find the school resistant. But no mortal can live in a state of complete self-deception either, and that irreducible kernel of self-knowledge is the school's natural ceiling.

## What You Can Do

- Conceal the true nature of something, obscuring what it is from perception and detection.
- Corrupt and warp material properties, accelerating decay or pulling something away from its original state.
- Corrupt magical workings such as wards and enchantments, destabilizing or warping their function.
- Warp existing things into unexpected states, using corruption as a form of discovery.

## What You Cannot Do

- Shadow magic cannot create anything new. It only acts on what already exists.
- Shadow magic cannot operate on the will of beings. It acts on the state of things, not the agency of people.
- Shadow magic cannot correct, fix, or heal in any conventional sense. It moves things away from their true state, never toward it.

## Features

- Access to shadow magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:elemental-magic -->
<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-orange-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-orange-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Incompatible with Illusion, Chaos, Light, and Shadow magic.<br>Mytts cannot use elemental magic.</span></div>

Elemental magic is not a school in the traditional sense. It is a framework, a way of understanding and directing magical energy through the lens of the natural world. Where other practitioners think in terms of schools and properties, elemental mages think in terms of fire, water, earth, wind, and lightning. That worldview is not a limitation. It is the mechanism. Magical energy channeled through that perspective takes on elemental expression, and the results are just as real and powerful as anything produced through conventional magical theory. Its themes are the forces of nature, the raw and physical world, and the power available to those who learn to speak its language rather than the language of academia.

Elemental magic existed as practice and tradition long before it actually worked. Rituals, symbols, the belief that the elements could be called upon and directed, none of it had any real weight behind it. Now the candle flame responds. The rain comes when called. The earth moves. What was superstition became something else entirely, and the practitioners who spent their lives devoted to those traditions found themselves ahead of everyone who was starting from scratch.

## What You Can Do

- Manipulate fire, calling it into being, shaping it, directing it, or extinguishing it.
- Influence and direct water, from ambient moisture to bodies of water.
- Move and shape earth, stone, and soil.
- Call and direct wind, from gentle currents to powerful gusts.
- Summon and direct lightning as a natural force.
- Combine elements for more complex effects, the way nature itself combines them.

## What You Cannot Do

- Elemental magic cannot create matter. Fire needs fuel. Water needs a source. Earth needs to be present. The elements are directed, not conjured from nothing.
- Elemental magic cannot be combined with illusion, chaos, light, or shadow magic. The worldviews are fundamentally incompatible.
- Elemental magic is not storm magic. Concentrating energy is not the same as calling fire.

## Features

- Access to elemental magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

Druidic magic is not a school in the traditional sense. Magical energy channeled through that perspective takes on a natural expression, and the results are as real and powerful as anything produced through conventional magical theory. Its themes are the natural world, the cycle of life and death, the bond between practitioner and living things, and the power available to those who have spent their lives in communion with nature rather than in study of it.

Druidic practice existed long before it actually worked. Herbalists, grove tenders, those who read the behavior of animals to predict the weather, those who performed seasonal rituals to encourage a good harvest, none of it had real weight behind it.
