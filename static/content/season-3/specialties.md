<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-red-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-red-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Only vampires can use blood magic.</span></div>

Blood magic is rooted in life force, the vital energy that animates living
things. It treats that energy as a transferable resource to be drawn,
redirected, and spent. It is the oldest school of magic, woven into the nature
of vampirism itself. For nearly 300 years it was the only magic in the world,
though it was not understood as a school at the time. People simply called it
vampiric power. It belongs, fundamentally, to the undead.

Its themes are life pushed past its natural limits. Sustenance and hunger. The
body as both tool and vessel. Preservation, corruption, and the fine line
between healing and harm. Every working comes at someone's expense. It is
intimate, parasitic, and extraordinarily powerful in the right hands.

All vampires can channel blood magic instinctively. It is woven into their
nature rather than learned, and basic applications come without instruction.
However, instinct is not mastery. Advanced applications such as fleshcrafting,
precise biological manipulation, and complex ritual work require dedicated study
and practice like any other school of magic.

<div class="p-4 rounded bg-background-800/60 border border-red-800/60 my-4">
  <span class="text-warm font-bold">Having blood magic innately is not the same as having the blood magic specialty.</span> All vampires can channel blood magic in combat and use it for basic purposes without a specialty. Notable use of blood magic in an RP context beyond standard in-game use does require the specialty. Innate ability and specialized skill are separate things.
</div>

## What You Can Do

- Transfer life force from a living source to fuel healing, sustain the caster,
  or power a working.
- Heal injuries by redirecting vitality into damaged tissue (with consent).
- Modify and reshape living flesh and biological systems with sufficient skill.
- Alter a living body in ways that push it beyond its natural state (RP only, no
  mechanical effects).
- Sustain life in conditions that would otherwise be fatal, through magical life
  support.
- Store life force in physical mediums and route it through objects and rituals,
  even when a mortal is handling it.

## What You Cannot Do

- Blood magic cannot generate life force from nothing. The conservation rule is
  absolute. What is spent must come from somewhere, and without a living source,
  there is nothing to work with.
- Blood magic cannot be channeled directly by a mortal. The barrier is
  structural, not a matter of training or talent. A mortal who attempts it
  suffers damage.
- Blood magic cannot resurrect the dead. Life force that has left a body cannot
  be forced back into it.

## Features

- Access to blood magic-themed abilities at each specialty level. Use the
  [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-green-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-green-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Only vampires and humans can use unholy magic.</span></div>

Unholy magic is the school of will and intent. Where other schools manipulate
forces or substances, unholy magic imposes the practitioner's purpose directly
onto magical energy, saturating it with their intent and turning it into a
carrier for what they want. The energy itself is irrelevant. The intent is
everything. Its themes are willpower, agency, the persistence of purpose, and
the residue that mortal minds leave behind in the world after death.

Graveyards, battlefields, places of worship, anywhere that intense mortal will
has been exercised over time, these locations accumulate will-saturated magical
energy that practitioners can draw on. The dead leave something behind too, not
souls, but fragments of who they were, echoes of a mind that once had will and
purpose, lingering in magically saturated ground and waiting to be compelled to
speak, at least for a time.

## What You Can Do

- Animate non-living matter by imprinting it with magical energy saturated with basic
  instructions and intent.
- Compel the mental fragments of the dead to manifest and interact, drawing on
  the residual imprint of their will and memory.
- Draw on environmentally accumulated will saturation in locations where mortal
  intent has soaked into the ground over time.
- Sustain workings through stored will-saturated magical energy, allowing
  effects to persist beyond active channeling.

## What You Cannot Do

- Unholy magic cannot control the living. An active will resists and adapts
  faster than any finite intent can suppress it.
- Unholy magic cannot conjure physical objects or create something from nothing.
  It operates on what already exists.
- Unholy magic cannot resurrect the dead. Fragments are residual imprints of
  will, not the person. Compelling one to speak is not bringing them back.
- Compelling a fragment consumes it. Extended interrogation uses up what
  remains. Once it is gone, it cannot be contacted again.

## Features

<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-yellow-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-yellow-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Mytts may take either Storm or Frost magic, but not both.</span></div>

Storm magic is the school of concentrated energy. It uses magical energy to
manipulate mundane energy or magical energy, gathering and focusing it against
its natural tendency to disperse. Its themes are concentration, accumulation,
intensity, and an intuitive attunement to how magical energy flows and pools in
the environment.

Storm magic is not lightning. It is not elemental in nature. Magical energy can
take a bolt-like form because it is the purest way raw energy can physically
express itself in the world, not because it shares anything fundamental with
mundane electricity. Storm practitioners develop a natural sense for energy
movement, sensing where it is concentrated, depleted, or shifting. This tends to
make them exceptional at rituals and artificing, where understanding how magical
energy flows through a working is as important as the working itself.

## What You Can Do

- Concentrate and direct raw magical energy for destructive or constructive
  purposes.
- Sense and analyze magical energy flow in the environment.
- Detect latent enchantments and map energy patterns in magical structures.
- Store concentrated magical energy in physical mediums such as topaz for later
  use.
- Push toward converting magical energy to mundane energy or the reverse, though
  this is genuinely unnatural and the consequences are unpredictable and
  potentially catastrophic.

## What You Cannot Do

- Storm magic cannot create energy from nothing. It works with what already
  exists, concentrating what is present rather than generating new energy.
- Storm magic cannot produce its effects without a source to draw from. Without
  energy present, there is nothing to concentrate.
- Storm magic cannot sustain concentrated energy indefinitely without effort or
  a storage medium. The world constantly pushes back toward equilibrium.

<div class="mb-4 px-4 py-2.5 rounded bg-background-700/60 border border-cyan-800/60"><span class="text-xl font-cinzel font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-cyan-500 block mb-1">Restriction</span><span class="text-base text-tprimary-50">Mytts may take either Storm or Frost magic, but not both.</span></div>

Frost magic is the school of energy depletion. It uses magical energy to
manipulate mundane energy or magical energy, removing it from a target or area
and causing cold and ice as the physical consequence of that removal. Frost
practitioners do not create elemental cold. They remove energy, which decreases
heat. Its themes are depletion, deficit, the world's constant push toward
equilibrium, and the structural knowledge gained through breaking things.

Every frost effect is a temporary state of imbalance that the world is
constantly trying to correct, requiring continuous channeling to maintain.
Magical energy fuels the removal process, spending itself to pull mundane energy
out of a space the way a pump spends effort to move water. Practitioners can
construct physical structures out of ice by removing energy from ambient
moisture, causing spontaneous condensation and freezing through shaped areas of
low energy. Ice pillars, barriers, and platforms are byproducts of that deficit,
not acts of creation. Frost practitioners can also target magical energy
directly, allowing them to influence flow through wards and magical constructs,
though this is more demanding and less predictable than working with mundane
energy.

## What You Can Do

- Remove mundane energy from a target or area to produce cold and ice as a
  physical consequence.
- Shape areas of low energy to produce ice structures such as barriers,
  platforms, or supports.
- Target magical energy in wards and constructs to disrupt or analyze their
  structure.
- Store energy vacuums in sapphires for later deployment without active
  channeling.

## What You Cannot Do

- Frost magic cannot create energy or add energy to a system. It only removes.
- Frost magic cannot produce permanent effects without a storage medium. Every
  effect requires ongoing channeling or it collapses as the world equalizes.
- Frost magic cannot create or repair anything directly. Ice structures are
  consequences of energy removal, not acts of creation.

Illusion magic is the school of creation through comprehension. It manifests
anything the practitioner truly understands, and the limit is never power but
always the depth of that understanding. Its themes are creation, comprehension,
the relationship between knowledge and reality, and the impermanence of what any
mortal mind can truly know.

Illusions are not phantoms or tricks of perception. They are tangible and real
while they exist. An illusory sword can cut. An illusory shield can block. But
no mortal practitioner can fully comprehend anything at the level reality itself
does, which means every creation is incomplete, a reflection of what the
practitioner imagines something to be rather than what it truly is. The moment
concentration breaks, the creation dissolves. Imperfections are not a sign of
poor skill. They are the nature of the school. What one imagines something to be
is never exactly what it is, and that gap is always visible in the work.

## What You Can Do

- Manifest temporary tangible objects, structures, or effects from
  comprehension.
- Create unique effects drawn from imagination and imposed on a target.
- Produce physical consequences through created things, damage from an illusory
  blade, shelter from an illusory wall.
- Create increasingly accurate and complex manifestations through deeper study
  and understanding of what you are creating.

## What You Cannot Do

- Illusion magic cannot create permanently. All manifestations dissolve when
  concentration breaks or the practitioner stops channeling.
- Illusion magic cannot destroy directly. Destruction is a side effect of what
  is created, not something the magic itself does.
- Illusion magic cannot control or influence the will and minds of others
  directly.
- Imperfect understanding produces imperfect results. A creation is only ever as
  complete as the practitioner's comprehension of it.

subject to chaos magic stop existing entirely, with no residue or remnant left
behind. Its themes are destruction, the removal of what exists, the discipline
of precision, and the weight of wielding something that cannot be undone.

Chaos fire is not chemical fire. It is a visual artifact of something being
removed from reality. It is not hot. It does not burn in the conventional sense.
It does not spread to adjacent material the way normal fire does. It is simply
what erasure looks like.

At its furthest reaches, chaos magic can push beyond physical matter into
something more dangerous, potentially unmaking concepts, ideas, or other things
that exist without having a physical form. This is not a school that forgives a
wandering mind. An unfocused practitioner does not simply fail. They erase the
wrong thing. This is a school that carries real weight, and we will treat it
accordingly.

## What You Can Do

- Erase physical matter, objects, and structures entirely with no residue.
- Destroy magical workings, dismantle wards, and destabilize enchantments.
- Push toward unmaking concepts or non-physical things at the extreme upper
  limit of the school.

## What You Cannot Do

- Chaos magic cannot create, construct, repair, or add anything to the world. It
  only removes.
- Chaos magic cannot reverse what it has done. Erasure is permanent.
- Chaos magic cannot function without extraordinary mental discipline. An
  unfocused working does not fail safely. It erases what it touches rather than
  what was intended.

things, and those two functions are the same thing seen from different angles.
To reveal what is true is already a partial restoration, because concealment and
corruption are departures from truth. Pushing something back toward what it
truly is and exposing what it truly is are the same motion. Its themes are
truth, revelation, restoration, clarity, and the cost of self-deception.

Light magic healing does not impose a new arrangement on a wounded body. It
pushes the body back toward what it naturally should be, restoring it toward its
true state. The school can also be used offensively, its physical manifestation
deployed as a weapon like any other magical school, but that is a product of war
rather than the nature of the magic itself. A practitioner's connection to the
school is directly tied to their own relationship with truth. Those who live in
self-deception, who hide who they are from themselves, will find the school
resistant and difficult. No mortal can live in a state of perfect truth, and
that natural limitation is the school's ceiling.

## What You Can Do

- Reveal the true state of things, piercing concealment, illusion, and disguise
  (with consent).
- Restore things toward their natural true state, including healing injuries by
  pushing the body back toward what it should be (with consent).
- Dissolve shadow magic corruption and other effects that have pulled something
  away from its true nature.
- Deploy light magic offensively as a physical manifestation, though this runs
  against the school's fundamental character.

## What You Cannot Do

- Light magic cannot restore death. The true state of a living thing does not
  persist after death in a form the school can act on.
- Light magic cannot compel truth from a person or influence their will. It acts
  on the state of things, not the agency of people.
- Light magic cannot restore what no longer exists. If the true state has been
  destroyed rather than concealed or corrupted, there is nothing to push back
  toward.

something truly is and warps it away from its true state, and those two
functions are the same thing seen from different angles, just as revealing and
restoring are their opposite. Its themes are concealment, corruption, decay,
secrets, and the willingness to dwell in what is hidden.

Shadow magic does not create anything new. It acts entirely on what already
exists, obscuring true nature or pulling it away from its original state. At its
most interesting it becomes a tool of discovery, warping what exists into states
the practitioner could never have imagined, finding what lies on the other side
of corruption rather than aiming for a predetermined outcome. A practitioner's
connection to the school is tied to their comfort with the hidden and obscured.
Those who demand clarity and insist on seeing things as they truly are will find
the school resistant. But no mortal can live in a state of complete
self-deception either, and that irreducible kernel of self-knowledge is the
school's natural ceiling.

## What You Can Do

- Conceal the true nature of something, obscuring what it is from perception and
  detection.
- Corrupt and warp material properties, accelerating decay or pulling something
  away from its original state.
- Corrupt magical workings such as wards and enchantments, destabilizing or
  warping their function.
- Warp existing things into unexpected states, using corruption as a form of
  discovery.

## What You Cannot Do

- Shadow magic cannot create anything new. It only acts on what already exists.
- Shadow magic cannot operate on the will of beings. It acts on the state of
  things, not the agency of people.
- Shadow magic cannot correct, fix, or heal in any conventional sense. It moves
  things away from their true state, never toward it.

way of understanding and directing magical energy through the lens of the
natural world. Where other practitioners think in terms of schools and
properties, elemental mages think in terms of fire, water, earth, wind, and
lightning. That worldview is not a limitation. It is the mechanism. Magical
energy channeled through that perspective takes on elemental expression, and the
results are just as real and powerful as anything produced through conventional
magical theory. Its themes are the forces of nature, the raw and physical world,
and the power available to those who learn to speak its language rather than the
language of academia.

Elemental magic existed as practice and tradition long before it actually
worked. Rituals, symbols, the belief that the elements could be called upon and
directed, none of it had any real weight behind it. Now the candle flame
responds. The rain comes when called. The earth moves. What was superstition
became something else entirely, and the practitioners who spent their lives
devoted to those traditions found themselves ahead of everyone who was starting
from scratch.

## What You Can Do

- Manipulate fire, shaping it, directing it, or extinguishing it.
- Influence and direct water, from ambient moisture to bodies of water.
- Move and shape earth, stone, and soil.
- Call and direct wind, from gentle currents to powerful gusts.
- Summon and direct lightning as a natural force.
- Combine elements for more complex effects, the way nature itself combines
  them.

## What You Cannot Do

- Elemental magic cannot create matter. Fire needs fuel. Water needs a source.
  Earth needs to be present. The elements are directed, not conjured from
  nothing.
- Elemental magic cannot be combined with illusion, chaos, light, or shadow
  magic. The worldviews are fundamentally incompatible.
- Elemental magic is not storm magic. Concentrating energy is not the same as
  calling fire.

genuine power, and those who had lived closest to nature found themselves with
an understanding of what they were doing that newcomers could not easily

expression. The profession is built on an intimate understanding of castle
magics and the tools to push them beyond their ordinary limits.

## RP Themes

- Structural integrity and the science of load-bearing construction.
- Designing and constructing buildings, fortifications, and interior spaces.
- Analyzing structural strain, weaknesses, and failure points in existing
  constructions.
- Aesthetic vision and the art of making functional spaces feel like something
  more.
- The tension between permanence and impermanence, building for the ages or
  building to last only as long as you need.

## Features

- Access to a custom set of modding features via chat commands, allowing you to
  spend coin to spawn decorative furniture from around the world. See the
  [Architect Commands](#cmd-architect) section for command syntax and usage.
- Place spawned furniture freely in free build mode.
- Move objects in free build mode with much higher granularity than normal.
- All architect abilities are scoped to castle territory bounds.
- At rank 3, you may expand a castle heart's floor limit by up to 300 bonus
  tiles. Each use adds +5 tiles and costs resources. The bonus is per castle
  heart and stacks on top of the base heart tier limit.

To be a blacksmith is to understand the steel in your hands and the
possibilities hidden within it. Where others see a finished weapon, a blacksmith
sees what it could become with the right reshaping. The forge is not just a
workplace. It is where intention meets metal.

## RP Themes

- Weapon design, modification, and the pursuit of the perfect blade.
- Metal crafting in all its forms, from precision components to crude structural
  parts.
- Shaping raw materials into functional and decorative forms through forging,
  tempering, and annealing.
- The relationship between smith and forge, discipline, heat, and timing.
- Reputation and craftsmanship, the weight of a name stamped into steel.

## Features

- Access to modded commands that allow you to change the weapon ability on the
  Q, E, and auto-attack slots of a weapon. Higher blacksmithing levels unlock
  more slots: level 1 grants the Q slot, level 2 grants the E slot, and level 3
  grants the auto-attack slot.
- Abilities placed on a weapon must follow handedness, which ensures the ability
  looks appropriate on the weapon.
- Changing weapon abilities costs materials.
- Assign a custom name to an item, displayed when someone uses the inspect
  command, equips the weapon, or uses a weapon emote with it.
- Weapons forged by a blacksmith can normally only be reforged by that same
  blacksmith. Blacksmiths may toggle this restriction off for weapons they have
  forged.
- See the [Blacksmithing Commands](#cmd-blacksmithing) section for command
  syntax and usage.

To be a tailor is to understand that what someone wears is never just clothing.
It is statement, identity, and sometimes armor in its own right. Where others
see fabric, a tailor sees silhouette, drape, and the quiet authority of a
well-cut cloak.

## RP Themes

- Fashion as identity, what a person chooses to wear and what it says about
  them.
- The craft of cutting, sewing, and finishing garments from raw cloth.
- Understanding material, weave, weight, and how fabric behaves on the body.
- Commission work and the social dynamic of dressing the powerful.
- The tension between function and form, clothing that must perform versus
  clothing that must impress.

## Features

- Access to custom command-based crafting recipes for all aesthetic cloaks and
  hats in the game. See the [Crafting Commands](#cmd-crafting) section for
  command syntax and usage.
- Reinforce armor to significantly increase its maximum durability. Once
  reinforced, that armor piece can only be repaired by a tailor (not necessarily
  the same one). Normal armor can still be crafted and repaired by anyone. See
  the [Tailoring Commands](#cmd-tailoring) section for command syntax and usage.

<!-- section:alchemy -->

To be an alchemist is to understand that the world can be reduced to its
properties and recombined into something greater than its parts. Where others
see herbs, minerals, and fluids, an alchemist sees potential reactions waiting
to happen. The laboratory is not just a workspace. It is where knowledge of what
things are becomes the power to change what they do.

## RP Themes

- Reagent knowledge, understanding the properties of herbs, minerals, and
  organic compounds.
- Brewing, distilling, and the meticulous craft of preparing reagents into
  useful compounds.
- Experimental methodology, the discipline of testing, observing, and refining
  results.
- The pursuit of transformation, turning the ordinary into the extraordinary
  through process and knowledge.
- Toxicity and restraint, the knowledge of what can kill and the judgment not to
  use it carelessly.

## Features

- Access to alchemy-themed abilities at each specialty level, such as a thrown
  healing potion or a thrown fire potion. Use the
  [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities) to assign abilities to your spell slots.

<!-- section:ritualism -->

To be a ritualist is to understand that magic does not simply happen. It is
guided, shaped, and given structure through ceremony. Ritualism is not tied to
any single magical school. It is the expertise in the craft of rituals
themselves, knowing how to organize and construct the space, how to place
objects with purpose, how to speak the right words and perform the right actions
to channel magical energy toward a desired goal. It is magic type agnostic. The
ritualist may not wield the fire, but they know how to build the hearth that
makes it burn brighter.

<div class="p-4 rounded bg-background-800/60 border border-purple-800/60 my-4">
  <span class="text-warm font-bold">Ritualism is not a replacement for a magical school.</span> Ritualism is not about channeling magic. With ritualism alone, you can perform only very, very small rituals. A ritual that involves a specific type of magic requires that magical school specialty. Ritualism by itself is not enough.
</div>

## RP Themes

- Ceremony and the weight of doing things the right way, in the right order, at
  the right time.
- Sacred and profane spaces, the construction of ritual circles, altars, and
  prepared ground.
- Symbolism and the power of objects, words, and gestures as focal points for
  magical intent.
- Collaborative magic, the role of the ritualist in coordinating and amplifying
  the work of multiple practitioners.
- Tradition and invention, the tension between rituals passed down and rituals
  discovered through necessity.

## Features

- Your ritualism level is taken into account alongside your magical school
  specialties when determining how successful you are at a ritual.
- Access to custom chat-based crafting recipes associated with Witchdust, a
  ritual-empowering material.
- Access to a series of toggleable visual buffs for aesthetic purposes to
  enhance ritual scenes.

instrument. It is discipline, repetition, and the pursuit of physical
excellence, sharpening what you were born with until it becomes more than
enough. A martial artist may also practice magic. This is not a rejection of the
arcane. It is its own discipline, focused on the physical, standing alongside
magic rather than against it. Where others reach for spells and steel, the
martial artist reaches inward.

- Discipline and the long road of training, repetition, and refinement.
- Close-quarters combat and the intimacy of fighting without distance or
  intermediary.
- The philosophy of the body, pushing physical limits through form, breath, and
  will.
- Style and tradition, the lineage of techniques passed from master to student.
- Restraint and precision, the ability to end a fight in a single strike and the
  wisdom to know when not to.

## Features

- Access to a selection of melee combat abilities at each specialty level,

To be a doctor is to understand the body as something that can be studied,
diagnosed, and treated through knowledge rather than sorcery. A doctor may use
magical materials in their practice, but doctoring itself does not require the
channeling of magic. It is a discipline of skill, observation, and steady hands
applied to flesh and bone.

Proper doctoring RP, treating wounds, performing procedures, and providing
medical care, is hard-scoped to the doctoring specialty. Picking someone up
because they are in your clan is not being a doctor. Handing someone a health
potion is not being a doctor. The specialty exists because the work demands it.

## RP Themes

- Diagnosis and the practiced eye, reading symptoms and knowing what the body
  needs.
- Surgery and intervention, the weight of cutting into another person to save
  them.
- Triage and composure under pressure, deciding who gets help first when not
  everyone can wait.
- The line between medicine and poison, knowledge that heals and knowledge that
  can kill.
- Consequences and failure, the reality that not every patient walks away.

## Features

- Individuals under the mortality contract can only receive medical RP from a
  specialist doctor. Use `.sov treat <player>` to treat a critical injury or
  `.sov revive` to revive a nearby downed player. See the
  [Doctor Commands](#cmd-doctor) section for details.
- Doctors can perform medical RP on themselves. Self-treatment follows the same
  rules as treating others.
- Use custom commands to revive someone or bring them off their knees without
  joining their clan, at the expense of material requirements.
- Expend material requirements to restore another person's blood pool amount.

To be a rancher is to understand that the living world is not just something to
pass through. It is something to be kept, tended, and drawn from with intention.
Where others see wild creatures only as obstacles or kills, a rancher sees
stock. Where others walk past a patch of blood rose, a rancher sees a yield. The
profession is built on capture and stewardship, taking wild things into your
keeping and harvesting from them over time rather than ending them in a single
moment.

A captured creature is beaten down, netted, dragged home, and penned. You then
tend it at your pace. The same discipline carries into the soil. A rancher reads
the harvest and the land gives back more for it.

## RP Themes

- Capture and the craft of the net, knowing what can be taken and what cannot.
- Stewardship of kept creatures, the steady work of tending stock over time.
- Harvest versus slaughter, drawing resources patiently rather than ending the
  animal.
- The bond between keeper and stock, responsibility for what you have taken into
  your keeping.
- Plant yield and the rancher's eye, reading what the land is offering and
  taking more from it.

## What You Can Do

- Capture weakened wild creatures with the Trapper's Net. The mod records the
  creature as a token. In character, you beat it down, net it, and drag it home.
- Spend a token to place that creature at your location, where it behaves as a
  normal wild animal would.
- Tend the animals near you to collect resources such as hide, bone, silkworm,
  and mutant grease, far more than you would get from killing the animal.
- Harvest any plant in the world for bonus resources of the same kind the plant
  naturally produces, scaling with your rancher level.

## What You Cannot Do

- You cannot capture players or player-owned creatures such as minions, summons,
  or familiars.
- You cannot capture a creature above your rancher tier. If the creature is too
  strong for you, your net snaps. If it cannot be captured at all, your net
  fails.
- You cannot keep more than 30 creatures per rancher.
- No castle may hold more than 30 creatures at once, no matter how many ranchers
  live there.
- This is not a Pokémon world. You do not keep creatures in your pocket to throw
  out on demand.
- The word "token" is a mod term only. In character, a creature never becomes a
  token and never becomes a small item you carry.
- You cannot spawn a creature out of thin air in character. Spawning a creature
  at random is not coherent and counts as abusing the specialty.
- In character, a capture looks like this: you beat the creature down, throw a
  net over it, and drag it home. You then keep it in a pen or stable on your
  land.

## Features

- The Trapper's Net is an ability. Use the [Abilities Commands](#cmd-abilities)
  to assign it to your spell slots.
- Access to rancher chat commands for capturing, placing, tending, and managing
  your animals. See the [Rancher Commands](#cmd-rancher) section for
  command syntax and usage.
