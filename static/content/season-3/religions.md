## The Great Temple of Qalidran

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The domain of the Second Descendant, the Temple of Qalidran is the core pillar of the island's official faith. While there is no official ban upon other religions within Qalidran, it is not common. It is certainly not done in the open. Outward service to a god or divine that is not the Progenitor results in a quick loss of power and influence for those bold enough to make their faith known.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### What the Temple Preaches

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Temple preaches thus:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>The Progenitor is a god. The only god.</li>
      <li>Vampires possess a spark of his divinity in their blood.</li>
      <li>Their domain over blood is divine in its origin; and blood is the holiest of magics.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

### The Descendants

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Descendants, who contain the most of his blood essence &ndash; his divine power &ndash; are openly revered as demi-gods. They are considered to be a step above the rest of vampirekind. They are the Progenitor's chosen &ndash; his mouth, his arms, and his hands.</p>
    <p>The Temple speaks his Word.</p>
    <p>The Harvest's hand holds his Home.</p>
    <p>The Parlor's hand grasps his Will.</p>
    <p>The arms of the Navy and the Guard protect all within his Embrace.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Temple Services

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Temple services occur twice a year. The Second Descendant reads from the Scarlet Tome &ndash; said to be a holy book that carries all of the Progenitor's lessons. Her sermons are long, usually lasting several hours, and are elaborate. Oftentimes, these ceremonies include stageplay-esque reenactments of the nation's founding mythos and several hymns.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### The Scarlet Siblings

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The average vampire who joins the Temple in service is referred to as a "scarlet brother/sister/sibling". The Scarlet Siblings wear all red, just as their Inner Palace counterparts do, but never wear masks. Scarlet Siblings work most commonly in conjunction with members of the Harvest to disseminate blood amongst the Fringes and the Garden. They also spread the words written in the Scarlet Tome and frequently give street-side lectures or sermons on the Progenitor's words.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Priests and Priestesses

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Proper, high-ranking Priests and Priestesses of the Temple are chosen exclusively from the Inner Palace. They oversee funerals, weddings, turnings, and other small ceremonies. They always wear all red and are masked. Their status is denoted by elaborate red lace veils and hoods. As their outfits are identical, it is impossible to say who one may be beneath that garb&hellip;</p>
    <p>Impersonation of a Priest(ess) is punishable by 50 years in the 5th Hell.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Belief in the Afterlife

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Qalidran vampires believe that upon death their blood essence returns to the Progenitor. However, because during their unlife their souls became one with their vampiric essence, they believe that there is true immortality to be found within his veins and the veins of all vampirekind.</p>
    <p>Qalidric funerals often involve pyres for the deceased, where objects they favor are burned, and then their bodies' ashes are added once the pyre is reduced to coals. The collective ashes are entombed in urns. Then, they are either carried by loved ones, or entombed in one of the city's handful of mausoleums. It is not uncommon to have very large, elaborate urns if the vampire was particularly wealthy.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Common Practices

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-red-800/60 to-red-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-red-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <ul>
      <li>Blood letting</li>
      <li>Blood sacrifices; usually vampire hunters who were captured in the outside world who are killed in ceremonies at sea by the Navy or during large sermons by the Temple.</li>
      <li>Twice annual temple rites.</li>
      <li>Weddings involve the tying of two vampire's wrists together with a scarlet ribbon and having the couple exchange blood. The 'red thread of fate' thus binds them together forever more.</li>
      <li>Turning someone into a vampire is considered giving them a piece of divinity. As such, the process is usually overseen by several members of the Temple.</li>
      <li>Risen vampires are also believed to be of the Progenitor's make, but as they are not directly related to him, they are seen as carrying little to none of his divine blood essence.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Luntoll

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p class="italic text-tprimary-400">"It is by the Mercy of the Moon that we survive. It is in the Curse of the Sun that we burn."</p>
    <p>Predating the rise of more dominant religions elsewhere in the world, the faith of Luntoll has been around longer than the Empire of Sul'uun itself. Luntollism has always had a chokehold on the central region of the eastern continent. As Luntollites shun &ndash; or outright persecute &ndash; followers of other religions, other predominant faiths have struggled to find a foothold in Luntoll lands.</p>
    <p>The faith of Luntoll is a dual one: both in service of the Moon and the Sun.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Worship and Beliefs

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The celestial bodies are personified as a feminine moon and a masculine sun, but the faith's iconography does not depict them in forms other than those they take in the sky. Their heavenly bodies are believed to be beyond mortal comprehension, and they do not appear in the forms of man in any of the religion's major texts.</p>
    <p>It is believed by Luntollites that the First Emperor's lack of service to the Sun is what wrought a curse upon their lands. As such, the faith is one centered around a balanced worship. They fear the wrath of the Sun, yes &ndash; but they also fear a world in which the Moon's mercy abandons them. Offerings, rituals, and ceremonies are done in two parts to ensure equality between the two.</p>
    <p>The sun's curse, which has rendered their land barren, is very real, though the actual origin has not been rigorously studied.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Weddings

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Luntollites consider weddings to be the single most important event in a person's life. Weddings are held at sunset or sunrise, when the divine couple shares the sky. Weddings held during the day or night are considered to be doomed at their onset. People in Luntoll are not considered "complete" until they have been joined with another in a divine marriage.</p>
    <p>Weddings are incredibly large ceremonies, and often involve entire villages in their production. One partner represents the Moon, and the other represents the Sun for the duration of the proceedings. However &ndash; it is not always the male spouse who represents the Sun or vice versa. Instead, the wealthier family's child is the one who wears gold adornments, while the poorer of the two is wrapped in silver splendor to pay homage to the Moon. This practice dates back to the earliest royal marriages at the Empire's onset.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Belief in the Afterlife

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Luntollites believe that they will join the Divine Couple in the sky after death &ndash; as one of the millions of stars that shines overhead. It is believed that the stronger one's faith, the brighter the star. In the Heavens there is no burning heat, or freezing night. Only peace and prosperity.</p>
    <p>Those who live faithless lives are cast into the darkness of the sky. Those cast into darkness will once again burn as the sun rises, and shiver in the darkness in the light of the moon. Tormented, until the rest of time, by their lack of personal light.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Common Practices

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <ul>
      <li>Huge weddings that occur either at dusk or dawn.</li>
      <li>Dual offerings to the sun and moon. Incense, burning plants/flowers, burying food, spilling water, and blood letting are common ceremonies.</li>
      <li>Extreme prejudice against other faiths.</li>
      <li>Extreme devotees may not consume anything that is not "of the desert"; choosing only to eat what little vegetation is native to the region and wyrm meat/blood.</li>
      <li>Married couples exchange a single earring from a pair that they each provide; one offering gold and the other silver. As such, a married person of the Luntoll faith dons one gold earring and one silver earring at all times. These earrings are permanent jewelry, and are usually welded closed as a part of the ceremony. Widowed Luntollites will don both sets of earrings, carrying their spouse with them until death.</li>
      <li>Luntollites are cremated upon death. At noon, funeral parties traverse into the desert. They travel as far as they can out of their villages during the day. The ashes are scattered once the moon, the sun, and the stars are visible. Funeral proceedings end once the sun has fully set.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

### Iconography

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-gold-800/60 to-gold-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-gold-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Sun and gold representing the masculine, Moon and silver representing the feminine. Eclipses represent marriages. The most common symbol is one golden hand and one silver hand, shown either palm to palm or holding each other.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The Faith of the Fish

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Fish is the Glorious Origin of All Life. It is the Cornerstone of all Existence. The Fish is the Loadbearing Fin on which All Life Rests.</p>
    <p>It is upon its scales that the continents are resting. It is within the fish's veins that the seas and rivers move. It is by the Fish's mercy that we live and we die. It is the Fish's breath that we breathe; it is the Fish's magic that we cast.</p>
    <p>Those who worship the Fish believe that the world itself is a single, divine fish. The stars beyond our atmosphere are the ocean in which our Divine Fish swims. We can see the current of it in dark nights: the current of thick stars that is currently swam upon.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Signs and Worship

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Fish gives the faithful signs in their ability to catch The Fish; a small icon of its presence. A fish that cannot be crushed or eaten. A small piece of the divine. Worshipers take these fish and build altars to their greatness; some constructing entire estates for the fish to live within.</p>
    <p>A fish worshiper almost always has fishtanks in their home. A large aquarium full of The Fish is a status symbol for their religious leaders: Anchors; who spread the good word of the Divine Fish.</p>
    <p>Fishermen are common followers of the faith. It is particularly prevalent in coastal and island communities all over the globe. However, worship of the Fish is said to date back to antiquity. Scholars in Frankleburg once found drawings of The Fish in an ancient cave. It is one of the oldest religions in the world.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Belief in the Afterlife

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Followers of The Fish believe that all life returns to the sea. Devout followers are reborn as perfect, immortal fish who are free to swim in any river, lake, or ocean their heart should choose.</p>
    <p>Those who led sinful, imperfect lives are believed to find reincarnation as man or beast upon the earth's surface. They will repeat this process until they get it right.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Common Practices

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <ul>
      <li>Offering wealth to the Sea; dumping valuables and gold into the depths of the sea to appease the Fish.</li>
      <li>Keeping elaborate aquariums and fishtanks.</li>
      <li>Eating lesser fish while offering thanks to the Fish. Some particularly devoted followers eat mostly fish and seaweed as they believe food is purest when it comes from the ocean. Food from the land is riddled with impurities.</li>
      <li>Rituals are usually done on beaches or out on the open water; both oceans and lakes.</li>
      <li>Pond ownership is a sign of devotion. Let The Fish into your home!</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

### Iconography

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>A gray fish, typically on a blue background. Blue and gray robes, lights, and decor are common among the faithful.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The Drift

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Everything is in flux. Nothing ever can remain as it was. There is no true state of things, the world itself is always in a state of becoming something else. Some people call this corruption, but those people are wrong. The true believers recognize it as the Drift. It is not celebrated, nor is it feared. It is existence itself.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Identity

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The biggest question of the faith is this: at what point does something cease to be what it once was? If you replace every plank of a ship, if a kingdom changes rulers a hundred times, if grief and sorrow completely transform a person, what is still the same? They have no answer for this. Instead it becomes the center of meditation and debate.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Corruption as Distance

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Corruption is not viewed as decay. It is viewed as the distance something has drifted. Everything accumulates distance from its origin. Some things go slowly, some catastrophically, some willingly and some unwillingly. A scar is corruption. Wisdom is corruption. Love and hate are corruption. None are inherently violent or wicked. They simply move someone further from who they are.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### The Physical Body

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The body is the easiest way that the Drift is observed. Scars, age, missing limbs, disease, strength, all evidence that things are not static. The faithful rarely pursue perfect magical healing because they believe scars have become part of someone who is now, not merely damage. This is not a pain cult. They do not go out hurting themselves.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### The Mind and Memory

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Memory is the only thing that can resist Drift. You cannot stop it. You cannot witness it. Families keep detailed history. Names are repeated across generations. Important objects are never made to look new; repairs stay visible. The past is not sacred, but forgetting the path of change is very dangerous.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Shadow Magic and the Drift

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Shadow mages are revered because they can actually cause something to Drift. Ordinary people and things change throughout the years. A shadow mage can deliberately change something. They are as much philosophers as they are mages to these practitioners. They are not priests. They are active observers. People who can ask and answer: if I move this further from what it is, what remains after?</p>
  </div>
</div>

### View on Vampires

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-violet-800/60 to-violet-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-violet-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The faithful are fascinated by vampires. They do not view them as monsters. They are the greatest unanswered question. If someone can become that different, where does identity end? Are they even the same person? Is the human in there somewhere? Has something new genuinely been created? There is no consensus. Some believe that vampires are humans stretched and drifted impossibly thin. Others believe that they are a new ascended being. The debates will likely never end.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Aetherian Tenets

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Less a formal religion and more a shared belief structure, the Aetherian Tenets are tied innately to the Talam. Each Talam feels a natural pull toward some of these beliefs, though they may fight against them or hold their own interpretation. Talam culture is heavily influenced by the cultures they originate from, and these beliefs can easily pass through the filter of other religions.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Existence Is a Series of Cycles

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Life and death go hand in hand. Predators eat prey. Forests burn then sprout new life. Fighting the cycles of life is foolish and is met with anything between annoyance, disappointment, or even aggression. Necromancy is an easy example of this violation, but others include seeking immortality, not allowing nature to reclaim its resources, or building a dam that disrupts an ecosystem.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### People and Nature Are One

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Everything exists within the cycles of life. People are no different. They are born, live, and die like everything else, but they have the ability to influence their environment more than any other creature. They may chop down a forest to build homes or hunt game to feed their families. So long as they help restore what was taken to keep the cycle going for their environment, all is well.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Nothing Is Set in Stone

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Even cycles themselves wax and wane. Perhaps a new creature is introduced to an environment, or a river redirects after a flood. Adaptation and acceptance of how life changes is equally important. Vampires have only existed for 300 years, but they have fit into the food chain just as humans did when they spread across the world.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Understanding Is Key

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-sky-800/60 to-sky-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-sky-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>Research and seeking information about the macro and micro of the world is important. How a local pollinator affects the bountiful plant life, which directly influences the prey population and the predator populations of an area. Perhaps with the surge of magic, suddenly ghosts roam and they disrupt that same forest's ecosystem. Finding a way to purge the undead by understanding the ways illusion and unholy magics caused such a thing is another form of this expression.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Faith of Shifting Eons

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Faith of Shifting Eons is the cycle of existence and thought. Not of life and death, but of activity and introspection, action and planning. A duality embodied by the Storm Speaker and the Frost Father.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Storm Speaker

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The one who brings change which breeds life in its wake. The one who calls for activity and energy. Thunder is their voice, rain is their influence, lightning is their excitement. So zealous is the Storm Speaker in showing their newest additions to the way of life that great tragedies are brought forth, be it from excitement, tests of faith and will, or both.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Frost Father

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The one who blankets the land for rest, who pushes people together, who calls for deep thought and introspection. Snow is his canvas, the still of night is his domain, the glee of families telling stories to one another bundled up in their homes is his joy. The Frost Father does not speak often, for he would rather listen. His rest readies the land, animals, and people for activity to come.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### The Balance

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>When both set forth their influence, the land and its people are given new purpose and revelations which will become tradition for some folk. Without both, the world would be in either a constant storm of endless change or a frozen state, unmoving. All would embody only one aspect and either burn themselves into a mess of incoherent words or simply lay down and barely rise but to sustain themselves. Those who follow this religion sometimes choose one to follow more than the other, but one way or another they too must have their cycles come full circle.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Common Practices

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <ul>
      <li>Making items to coincide with a promise or task, which upon completion are burned or tossed into the sea, marsh, or placed on the edge of a glacier.</li>
      <li>Listening to the sky as a form of weather or life forecasting.</li>
      <li>Cleansing of the home which involves moving furniture or removing items no longer needed.</li>
      <li>Meditative artwork done in places where the artwork will never stay, such as on sand, snow, or a shoreline.</li>
      <li>Rituals are done in a variety of places depending on which energy is meant to be called. Mountain tops during a lightning storm, fast rivers, or shorelines for high energy. Frozen landscapes, landmarks which have stood the test of time, and caves for low energy needs. Crossroads, marshlands, and forests for both.</li>

### Belief in the Afterlife

  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-blue-800/60 to-blue-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-blue-900/30"></div>

    <p>Those in this faith believe that upon death folk will be embraced by the Frost Father and enter a state of rest. One that will revitalize the soul to once again go and bring forth the Storm Speaker's energy and will into the world.</p>
    <p>If someone's body is preserved, even when their soul comes back from rest they will remember who they were before, and this will help them as they do their crucial work for the Frost Father and Storm Speaker. Though this can also be a shackle upon the soul if filled with too much woe and strife. The soul's work cannot be done free of burden and thus may be better released from previous mortal shackles.</p>
