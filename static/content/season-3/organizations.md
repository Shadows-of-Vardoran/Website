### How They Operate

<div class="flex gap-4 my-2">
  <div class="w-3 rounded bg-gradient-to-b from-slate-800/60 to-slate-950/80 flex-shrink-0 border border-slate-900/30"></div>
  <div class="flex-1 text-[1.2rem]">
    <p>The Legion has no central leadership, no hierarchy, no governing body making decisions from the top down. What it has is a chain of turning. Each vampire within the Legion holds authority over those they have personally turned, and those turned vampires hold authority over those they turn in turn. It spreads outward like a web with no center, which makes it nearly impossible to decapitate. There is no figure to remove, no command structure to collapse. Killing one node does not slow the rest.</p>
    <p>They recruit through kidnapping and indoctrination, bringing in mortals who have no interest in joining and leaving them with no path back out. Those who convert fully are turned. Those who do not serve a different purpose.</p>
