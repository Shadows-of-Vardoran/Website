# Religions

## Qariel

<details open>
<summary>Click to expand/collapse Qariel</summary>

<p><strong>The Flame that Feeds Itself – They who shape desire.</strong></p>

<p>
Qariel is not the god of any one domain. Qariel is the god of more. More blood. More devotion. More harvest. More steel, flesh, sound, hunger, gold, fire, purpose. To worship Qariel is to never stop, to never be satisfied, to sacrifice selfhood in the pursuit of perfection or indulgence—until that very pursuit devours the soul, leaving behind only a hollow echo that screams “again.”
</p>

<p>
A wailing choir of flayed monks, carving prayers into themselves with bone needles.<br>
A demon forged of molten teeth and gold, laughing as it eats warhorses and its riders.<br>
A perfectly silent potter, who remakes the same clay bowl ten thousand times in a dark cell.<br>
A king who never stops building, whose city is a spiral of towers too tall for light to reach.
</p>

<p>
Worship is not tied to what is done. Only that it is done again, and again, and again, until nothing else remains. Though daemons and violent cults are the most visible (and feared) of Qariel’s followers, they are far from the only ones.
</p>

<p>
The god's true cult is fractured, a collection of the obsessed and the devoted, all pursuing excess in different forms. The Daemons however remain the most prevalent. Demon summoning is as much a talent as it is a teachable skill.
</p>

<p>
Experienced summoners and mages often can tell how talented a new summoner will be simply by getting a sense of their personality. When a mage dies near a summoning circle, Qariel’s beasts may take the body as their own, warping bone, voice, and thought into a vessel of excess.
</p>

<p><em>No corpse lies still in the presence of a Daemon.</em></p>
</details>



## Ignariel

<details open>
<summary>Click to expand/collapse Ignariel</summary>

<p><strong>The Undying Flame</strong></p>

<p><strong>Domain:</strong> Life, Light, Order, Purity</p>
<p><strong>Symbol:</strong> A haloed flame within a sunburst</p>
<p><strong>Worshipped in:</strong> The East (Brightmoor), especially in the Gleaming Ward</p>

<p><strong>Overview:</strong><br>
Ignariel is the radiant deity of fire, birth, and unyielding vitality. Once one half of a balanced divine pair, he became ascendant through the fervent devotion of mortals who feared death.
</p>

<p><strong>Doctrine:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Death is an imperfection to be purged.</li>
<li>Eternal life is earned through devotion and discipline.</li>
<li>Flame cleanses and preserves the righteous.</li>
<li>Magic must be governed by divine order.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Legacy:</strong><br>
Led the faithful into conflict with the Brotherhood of Nymeris. A crusade he inspired ended in the mysterious Silence beneath the Necropolis. His prophet, Saint Velariun, was never seen again.
</p>
</details>

## Nakir’el

<details open>
<summary>Click to expand/collapse Nakir’el</summary>

<p><strong>The Moon God</strong></p>

<p><strong>Title:</strong> The Sleepless One, Shadowbinder, Keeper of Secrets<br>
<strong>Domains:</strong> Darkness, lies, dreams, vengeance<br>
<strong>Symbols:</strong> Dark crescent, black hole, owls<br>
<strong>Sacred Color:</strong> Silver &amp; Indigo<br>
<strong>Devotees:</strong> The Nakirathi, performing rites in moonlit ruins
</p>

<p>
Long ago, the Moon God and Sun Goddess danced in harmony. Mortals worshipped them both, until the Sun Goddess was favored for the life she brought.
</p>

<p>
In envy, the Moon God stole her light by causing an eclipse. Fear spread among mortals, and the Sun Goddess banished him from the sky.
</p>

<p>
Consumed by rage, the Moon God created creatures of the night—vampires—to become humanity’s greatest terror.
</p>
</details>
